# The one-car limit — art. 68 alin. (5) lit. j) is **not determinable** from primary sources

**Verified 2026-10-12.** Research performed by the Researcher agent (web-only, no repository access); this note is written by the orchestrator from its report.

**Why this was asked:** 6c needs to encode the vehicle rules. `research/22` line 152 recorded the treatment of **two or more cars** as UNVERIFIED. This pass tested whether it can be resolved.

---

## 1. Headline — the selection has no primary-source answer

**FACT.** Art. 68 alin. (5) lit. j) caps deductible running costs at „cel mult un singur autoturism aferent fiecărei persoane", but **neither the Cod fiscal nor the Normele metodologice (HG 1/2016 pct. 7) ever say which car that is**, how it is identified, or what happens to the others. There is no selection criterion, no election mechanism, no annual-designation rule, and no cross-reference to any register or declaration.

**FACT — and this is the strongest single piece of evidence.** The word **„autoturism" does not appear anywhere in pct. 7 of the Normele** — confirmed by full-text check of HG 1/2016 as published (MO 22/13.01.2016) and of ANAF's consolidated annotated edition with the Normele as amended through HG 602/2025. The Normele regulate only the 50 % haircut and the *foaie de parcurs*.

**FACT.** No official ANAF document states which car. ANAF's own brochures (DGRFP Galați/Vrancea „Cheltuieli deductibile – autoturisme"; DGRFP Iași „Material informativ" 27.01.2026) restate the rule and stop. **No court decision located decides which car.**

**UNKNOWN.** Whether the limit is taxpayer-chosen, per fiscal year, or determined by use/evidence. **The texts do not answer. Do not encode any of these as a rule.**

---

## 2. The provisions verbatim

Source: Cod fiscal (Legea 227/2015), **forma consolidată 08.08.2026** — Portal Legislativ act page `DetaliiDocument/283693`, consolidation `DetaliiDocument/312600`. Portal Legislativ did not render the body text in that environment, so the verbatim text is from **ANAF's official consolidated edition** (`Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm`, header „ultima actualizare: LEGEA nr. 170/2026"; Normele „Hotărârea Guvernului nr. 602/2025"), cross-checked word-for-word against a secondary mirror of the *same* 08.08.2026 version.

**alin. (5) lit. j):**
> „cheltuielile de funcționare, întreținere și reparații, aferente autoturismelor folosite de contribuabil sau membru asociat sunt deductibile limitat potrivit alin. (7) lit. k) **la cel mult un singur autoturism aferent fiecărei persoane**;"

Note the plural „autoturismelor" with a singular cap: **the drafter contemplated several cars and capped the allowance at one, without saying which.**

**alin. (7) lit. k)** — quoted in full in `research/21` §4.1. Two closing sentences matter here: „Cheltuielile care intră sub incidența acestor prevederi nu includ cheltuielile privind amortizarea." and **„În cazul vehiculelor … care nu fac obiectul limitării deducerii cheltuielilor, se vor aplica regulile generale de deducere."**

**alin. (7) lit. a)** — „sumele sau bunurile utilizate de contribuabil pentru uzul personal sau al familiei sale;" (fully non-deductible).

**Terminology (FACT).** „autoturism" is **not defined in art. 7 Cod fiscal**. alin. (5) lit. j) says „autoturism"; alin. (7) lit. k) says „vehicule rutiere motorizate" (MTMA ≤ 3.500 kg, ≤ 9 seats). The Normele for art. 28 use the **M1** category (RNTR 2, Ordin MLPTL 211/2003). **INTERPRETATION:** the one-car cap is a *passenger-car* cap; a light commercial vehicle caught by lit. k) is arguably not an „autoturism" for lit. j).

## 3. What the Normele say about identifying the car — nothing

Normele HG 1/2016 pct. 7 alin. (7) is quoted verbatim in `research/21` §4.1. Adding what matters here:

- **FACT.** pct. 7 contains **no** *foaie de parcurs* rule for identifying a car, no Registrul-inventar designation, no declaration, no election. The *foaie de parcurs* is a **proof-of-use** instrument for full deductibility, **not a selection device** — though it is per-trip and per-vehicle („categoria de vehicul utilizat"), so in practice it is the only primary-source document recording *which* vehicle was used for *which* trip.
- **FACT.** pct. 7 alin. (8): „toate bunurile, drepturile și obligațiile aferente desfășurării activității se înscriu în Registrul-inventar și constituie patrimoniul afacerii." So **registering both cars is mandatory and does not designate the deductible one.**
- **FACT.** The Registrul-inventar model for PFA (OMFP 170/2015, cod 14-1-2/b) has three columns only — nr. crt. / denumirea elementelor inventariate / valoarea de inventar — **no field designating a car**. *(Confirmed via secondary descriptions; the order text itself was not retrieved.)*
- **FACT.** The corporate analogue is equally silent: Normele for art. 25 alin. (3) lit. l)/m) restate „un singur autoturism aferent fiecărei persoane cu astfel de atribuții" with no identification rule.

---

## 4. The distinction the texts *do* support — and the gap that remains

**FACT.** alin. (7) lit. k) is **per vehicle and has no per-person cap.**

**FACT.** lit. j) is limited by its own words to „autoturismele **folosite de contribuabil** sau membru asociat". A car not used by the taxpayer or an associate member is, on the face of the text, **outside lit. j)**.

**INTERPRETATION — textually supported, and the most useful finding in the pass.** A car used **only for the activity / only by employees / only within a listed exception category** is not caught by the one-car cap: it is governed by alin. (4) + alin. (7) lit. k), i.e. **100 % deductible if exclusively used** (documents + *foaie de parcurs*), **50 % if mixed**. A car **used by the taxpayer personally** is inside lit. j), and only one such car may carry costs.

**UNKNOWN — the crux.** The law never says what happens to a **second** car that *is* used by the taxpayer. Two readings, neither stated:
1. Its running costs are **fully non-deductible** — they are not among the „deductibile limitat" items of alin. (5) once the one-car allowance is spent, and alin. (7) lit. a) „uzul personal" points the same way.
2. They remain deductible under alin. (4) **with the 50 % haircut**, because Normele pct. 7 alin. (7) applies the 50 % cap to „cheltuielile efectuate conform art. 68 alin. (4) **și (5)** … legate de aceste vehicule" with no selection rule.

**Do not pick one.**

**FACT — a real asymmetry worth encoding.** The depreciation cap is expressly **per vehicle with no one-car limit**: Normele for art. 28 alin. (14) — „pentru mijloacele de transport de persoane care au cel mult 9 scaune de pasageri … din categoria M1 … cheltuielile cu amortizarea sunt deductibile, **pentru fiecare**, în limita a 1.500 lei/lună." So **several M1 cars can each be depreciated up to 1.500 lei/lună while only one carries running costs.**

**SECONDARY, not to be encoded.** contapp.ro says that at a PFA with one owner and no employees the allowance is one car, „un al doilea autoturism se justifică prin existența salariaților sau prin încadrarea într-una dintre categoriile de excepție", and that „ANAF acceptă în practică deducerea pentru un singur autoturism per persoană implicată". **No ANAF document found supports this.**

**FACT — case law.** No decision decides *which* car. Decizia 772/14.10.2020 (Curtea de Apel) denied deduction for a personally-owned car used through a law office **for lack of proof of use** (foi de parcurs, ordine de deplasare) — a proof issue, not a selection issue. CCR Decizia 123/2011 upheld the corporate one-car rule as within the legislature's competence.

## 5. Per person, not per register

**FACT.** The cap is expressed **per person** — „aferent **fiecărei persoane**"; the corporate analogue „aferent fiecărei persoane cu astfel de atribuții". Nothing ties it to a CUI, a register or a business.

**FACT.** „membru asociat" is named, so for an *asociere fără personalitate juridică* the text supports one car **per member**, not per association.

**UNKNOWN.** A natural person holding PFA **and** II/IF simultaneously. „Fiecărei persoane" points to one car for the human being; nothing resolves it.

---

## 6. Order of application — cumulative, not alternative

**FACT.** The two rules are **cumulative**: lit. j) itself says the costs are „deductibile limitat **potrivit alin. (7) lit. k)** la cel mult un singur autoturism" — the one-car limit is expressed *through* the 50 % rule, not instead of it. Normele pct. 7 alin. (7) confirms, applying the 50 % cap to costs made „conform art. 68 alin. (4) **și (5)**".

**FACT.** The 50 % base **includes non-deductible VAT** — Normele pct. 16 alin. (3), same mechanism: „Aplicarea limitei de 50% … se efectuează după aplicarea limitării aferente taxei pe valoarea adăugată, respectiv aceasta se aplică și asupra taxei … pentru care nu s-a acordat drept de deducere".

**Sequence the texts support:** (1) is it an „autoturism" **folosit de contribuabil/membru asociat**? → only one such car may carry running costs; (2) for that car, exclusive use (documents + *foaie de parcurs*) or an exception category? → 100 %, else 50 %; (3) depreciation separately, **per vehicle**, art. 28 alin. (14). **Step (1) has no tie-breaker when there are two taxpayer-used cars.**

## 7. Secondary questions reached

- **Exception categories and evidence.** The income-tax Normele import the TVA concepts wholesale (pct. 7 alin. (7) → pct. 68 Normele for art. 298 alin. (6)). In the TVA Normele, for the corresponding categories, „utilizarea acestora și pentru uz personal fiind considerată **neglijabilă** cu excepția situației în care se poate face dovada unei practici abuzive" — i.e. no trip log there. **INTERPRETATION:** category membership is proved by the nature of the activity, not by a *foaie de parcurs*. **UNKNOWN** whether ANAF accepts that for income tax.
- **Leasing / rented-in cars are inside the cap.** alin. (7) lit. k) covers vehicles „aflate în **proprietate sau în folosință**"; the imported TVA Normele speak of „închirierii sau leasingului", and the corporate Normele list „chiriile" among the costs subject to 50 %. **INTERPRETATION:** a short-term rental is inside too — no carve-out; exception category 4 („închirierea **către alte persoane**") is renting *out*, not *in*. **Note:** the income-tax list has **five** categories while TVA art. 298 alin. (3) has **six**, including leasing-company fleets — **that TVA carve-out has no income-tax equivalent.**
- **Comodat** is not addressed; „folosință" is broad enough to cover it (INTERPRETATION).
