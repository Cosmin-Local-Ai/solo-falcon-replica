# R-CASS-CAS — Art. 170 / 174¹ / 151 verbatim for FY2026: the ladder is a base, not a percentage, and two citations in my own brief do not exist

**Researched by** `researcher-cass-cas-verbatim` on 2026-10-14, web only. **Written to file by the orchestrator** — the Researcher has no filesystem access (§12.16, §12.14). **Prompted by the Worker refusing to build a tested branch on an unreconciled percentage.**

## Why this note exists
In the Stage 4 brief I wrote: *„CASS — art. 174¹ alin. (1) 10 % of actual net; alin. (2) 40 % of the minimum contribution for cumulated ≥ 6 SMN; alin. (3) 80 % for ≥ 12 SMN; alin. (4) 100 % for ≥ 24 SMN; alin. (5) 10 % of actual net for ≤ 6 SMN"* and *„CAS — alin. (1¹) 12,5 % for pensioners whose income is under 80.000 lei"*.

**Those figures came from my prompt and exist nowhere in the corpus.** The Worker caught it arithmetically: at SMN 4.050 the base ladder gives 2.430 / 4.860 / 9.720, and **2.430/9.720 = 25 %, 4.860/9.720 = 50 % — not 40 % and 80 %.** A reading that cannot reproduce ANAF's own published *„bază 97.200 (24 SMN) → CASS 9.720"* is not the reading.

**This is the third wrong premise I put into a brief in this phase.** The first two are named in `research/48`.

## ★ Reading A is the law. Reading B has no textual basis.
**Art. 174¹ alin. (1), verbatim (FACT):**
> *„Contribuția de asigurări sociale de sănătate se calculează de către contribuabilii prevăzuți la art. 170 alin. (2) prin aplicarea **cotei de contribuție prevăzute la art. 156** asupra **bazei anuale de calcul menționate la art. 170 alin. (3)**."*

**Art. 170 alin. (3), verbatim (FACT)** — the ladder is a **base**, expressed only in SMN multiples:
> *„Baza anuală de calcul al contribuției … în cazul persoanelor care realizează venituri din cele prevăzute la art. 155 alin. (1) lit. c) – h) o reprezintă: **a) nivelul a 6 salarii minime brute** … cuprinse între 6 SMN inclusiv și 12 SMN; **b) nivelul de 12 salarii minime brute** … între 12 SMN inclusiv și 24 SMN; **c) nivelul de 24 de salarii minime brute** … cel puțin egale cu 24 SMN."*

**At SMN 4.050 lei: base 24.300 / 48.600 / 97.200 → CASS 2.430 / 4.860 / 9.720 lei.** Exactly ANAF's published table.

**40 % / 80 % / 100 % appears in none of art. 148, 151, 170, 174, 174¹.** **My re-attribution hypothesis — that the percentages are minimum floors on the independent-activity limb — is REFUTED.** Art. 170 alin. (1) contains **no floor at all**, only a ceiling.

## The real CASS minimum — a base floor, not a percentage (FACT)
**Art. 174 alin. (6)** — and it belongs to the **lit. b) / independent-activity limb**:
> *„În situația în care baza de calcul prevăzută la art. 170 alin. (1) … este mai mică decât cea corespunzătoare unei baze de calcul egale cu nivelul de **6 salarii minime brute** pe țară, persoanele fizice datorează contribuția … **până la nivelul celei corespunzătoare bazei de calcul egale cu 6 SMN** …"*

**Art. 174 alin. (7)** — the difference is **not** owed if the taxpayer also realised: **lit. a)** salary ≥ 6 SMN; **lit. b)** art. 155 lit. c)–h) income for which CASS is owed at ≥ 6 SMN; **lit. c) *„venituri din pensii, definite conform art. 99"*** — **lit. c) added by Legea 141/2025, from FY2025 incomes.**

**Every "minimum" in Titlul V is an SMN multiple, never a percentage of a reference contribution:** art. 148 alin. (2) and (4), art. 151 alin. (3), art. 180 alin. (2), art. 182¹. **The only percentage-of-a-contribution split found anywhere is art. 180 alin. (2) (Legea 141/2025): *„a) 25 % … la data depunerii declarației; b) 75 % … până la data de 25 mai inclusiv a anului următor"*** — **25/75, not 40/80/100. This is the most likely source of my confusion.**

## ⚠️ Art. 151 alin. (1¹) does not exist. Neither does 12,5 % CAS, nor an 80.000 lei threshold.
**Art. 151 alin. (1), verbatim (FACT):**
> *„Contribuabilii prevăzuți la art. 148 alin. (1) și (4) calculează contribuția de asigurări sociale **prin aplicarea cotei prevăzute la art. 138 lit. a)** asupra **bazei anuale de calcul menționate la art. 148 alin. (2)** …"*

**Art. 138 lit. a) = 25 %.** Art. 151 runs **alin. (1) → (16)** with **no indexed alineate**; alin. (14) is the *bonificație de până la 10 %*. **No 12,5 % rate and no 80.000 lei threshold in art. 151, 148, 150 or 138.** **CORROBORATED by a second consolidation (GhidFiscal.ro).** The 80.000 lei that appears in ANAF FAQ material is **an example income figure in a 2020 dividend case, not a legal threshold.**

**What the 12/24 SMN thresholds measure (art. 148 alin. (1)–(3), verbatim):** cumulated **venit net / normă anuală de venit** from **art. 137 alin. (1) lit. b) *și* b¹)** — activități independente (art. 68, 68¹, 69) **plus** DPI (art. 72, 72¹, 73) — **for the single fiscal year, across all sources *and* both categories. Per year, not per category.** Base = *„venitul ales de contribuabil"*, floor 12 SMN / 24 SMN.

## ⚠️ Renumbering — this is how a correct rule becomes a wrong citation (FACT)
**OUG 128/2024 (MO 1125/11.11.2024), applicable from 1 January 2025, rewrote both articles:**
- **The CASS ladder moved from art. 170 alin. (5) → art. 170 alin. (3).** The cumulation rule moved to alin. (4). **Any FY2026 brief citing „art. 170 alin. (5)" is citing pre-2025 numbering** — and `research/48` §5 does exactly that.
- **Art. 174¹ went from alin. (1)–(21) → alin. (1)–(12).**
- **The FY2024 floor limb, old art. 174¹ alin. (19)** (sub-6-SMN realised income still owed CASS on a 6-SMN base) **has no counterpart in the current text.** **Combined with art. 170 alin. (2) as quoted, from FY2025 a lit. c)–h) taxpayer whose *realised* cumulated income is under 6 SMN owes no CASS at all.** CORROBORATED by ANAF Galați's din oficiu procedure.
- **Legea 239/2025 raised the art. 170 alin. (1) ceiling from 60 → 72 SMN** (ANAF Brașov official before/after table, Titlul V pct. 18–20). **FY2026: 72 × 4.050 = 291.600 lei, CASS max 29.160 lei.**

## ★ Art. 135¹ alin. (3) — verified, and it is exactly what we assumed (FACT)
> *„În aplicarea prevederilor **art. 148, 151, 170, 174 și 174¹**, la verificarea încadrării venitului în plafoanele anuale, precum și la stabilirea bazei anuale de calcul … **se ia în calcul valoarea salariului minim brut pe țară … în vigoare la data de 1 ianuarie a anului de realizare a venitului** … **indiferent dacă în cursul aceluiași an se utilizează mai multe valori** ale salariului minim brut pe țară."*

**FY2026 operative SMN = 4.050 lei** (HG 1.506/2024; no HG took effect 1 Jan 2026). **HG 146/2026 (MO 196/13.03.2026) sets 4.325 lei from 1 iulie 2026** and abrogates HG 1.506/2024 at that date — **it governs FY2027 thresholds, not FY2026.** *(Amending act for art. 135¹ itself: UNVERIFIED — likely OUG 115/2023.)*

## The withholding state machines — corrected alineate for FY2026
**CASS withheld — art. 174¹ (current numbering):** **alin. (3)** single payer, estimated net ≥ **6 SMN** → payer calculates with art. 156 rate on the art. 170 alin. (3) base, withholds and pays; **alin. (4)** multiple sources → taxpayer *designates by contract* the payer whose income alone ≥ 6 SMN; **alin. (5)** that payer files **D112 (art. 147 alin. (1))** by the 25th; **alin. (6) the top-up:** withheld on a base below 12/24 SMN but cumulated ≥ 12/24 SMN → file D212 to establish the higher base, and *„contribuția reținută … **este luată în calcul**"*; **alin. (7)** the payer issues a non-standardised *informare*; **alin. (8)** each source under 6 SMN but cumulated ≥ 6 SMN → **no withholding**, taxpayer files the DU; **alin. (9)** other lit. c)–h) income → file the DU unless the withheld amount already equals the amount due.

**CAS withheld — art. 151 alin. (5)–(11)** per `research/48` §4: same shape, **trigger 12 SMN**, top-up to 24 SMN at alin. (9), no-withholding case at alin. (10), *informare* at alin. (11).
