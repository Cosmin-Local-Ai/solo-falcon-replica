## Q118-B — Composition of venitul net anual impozabil — HIGH

The app's `venit net anual − CAS − CASS` is **incomplete and mis-ordered**. Correct sequence,
art. 118 alin. (2):

1. **Per source:** `venit net anual recalculat = venit net anual (sistem real, art. 68) − pierderi
   fiscale reportate` — alin. (2) lit. a). **Losses come off here, before sources are summed.**
2. **Sum across sources:** `Σ venit net anual recalculat` — alin. (2) lit. b).
3. **Subtract CAS and CASS, each PRORATED** — never the full contribution:
   - `CAS deductibilă = (venit net anual în sistem real ÷ total venituri cumulate art. 148 alin. (3)) × CAS calculată art. 151 alin. (1)` — alin. (2^2)
   - `CASS deductibilă = (venit net anual în sistem real ÷ total venituri cumulate art. 170 alin. (1)) × CASS calculată art. 174 alin. (1)` — alin. (2^3)
4. **Hard cap — alin. (2^4):** `CAS deductibilă + CASS deductibilă ≤ venitul net anual recalculat`.
   The excess is simply not deductible.

**Direct answers:**
- **CAS is NOT deductible in full** — prorated by the art. 148 alin. (3) ratio, then constrained by alin. (2^4).
- **CASS:** the art. 174 alin. (6) difference is **excluded outright**, *and* the remainder is prorated
  by the art. 170 alin. (1) ratio. Two separate reductions, not one.
- **Regime split:** alin. (2) → **art. 61 lit. a)** (activități independente — the PFA case; CAS *and*
  CASS deductible). alin. (2^1) → **art. 61 lit. a^1)** (CAS only).

**Single-source consequence (important for this app):** a PFA whose only income is sistem-real
art. 68 income has ratio = `venit net ÷ venit net` = **1**, so the proration is a **no-op today**.
The mechanism must still be *named and structured*, because it stops being a no-op the moment a second
income source exists (Phase 6 profile fields), and because alin. (2^4) is **not** a no-op.

## Q118-C — Loss carry-forward — HIGH

- **(i) Where:** **art. 118 alin. (4), (5), (7)** — quoted verbatim above. **Not art. 68.**
- **(ii) Period:** **5 consecutive fiscal years** (was 7; shortened by OUG 115/2023 Pct. 70 Art. LIII
  Cap. II, MO 1139/15.12.2023, applicable from 2024 incomes).
- **(iii) Election:** none found in the text — „se reportează **și se compensează**" is mandatory.
  No „la cerere", no „opțiune". Chronological by age of loss; right is personal and non-transferable;
  unabsorbed loss after the period becomes a **definitive loss** of the taxpayer.
- **(iv) Same-source restriction:** yes — „**din aceeași sursă de venit**" (alin. (4)), and 70% cap.
- **(v) 2025/2026 amendment:** none (see article identity above).

## Q118-E — Minimum tax — clean negative

- There is **no turnover-based minimum tax for a 2026 PFA in sistem real**.
- The premise in the Phase 5 brief ("art. 10^1 / art. 118^1") is **mis-numbered**: art. 10^1 does not
  exist in the current consolidation at all. The real 1% „impozit minim pe cifra de afaceri" is
  **art. 40^1, Titlul II (impozit pe profit)** — a different title, so it does not reach Titlul IV
  sistem-real income regardless of any repeal history.
- Repeal history of the old article: **not located, and out of scope** — the negative stands without it.

## Q-REGIME — can a PFA be a CIT taxpayer at all? **No.** — MEDIUM-HIGH

**There is no exclusion article, because the exclusion runs the other way: Titlul II never reaches a PFA
in the first place.**

- **CF art. 14 lit. a), Titlul II, Cap. I („Sfera de cuprindere a impozitului”)** — verbatim:
  > „Impozitul pe profit se aplică după cum urmează: a) În cazul **persoanelor juridice** române, al
  > persoanelor juridice străine rezidente în România potrivit locului conducerii efective, precum și al
  > persoanelor juridice cu sediul social în România, înființate potrivit legislației europene, asupra
  > profitului impozabil obținut din orice sursă, atât din România, cât și din străinătate;"
  > *(last amended: Legea nr. 296/2020, Pct. 5, Art. I, **MO nr. 1269 din 21.12.2020**, eff. 01.01.2021)*

  A PFA is a *persoană fizică*, so it is never inside art. 14 lit. a).
- **Symmetrically, Titlul IV never lets that income escape:** art. 61 lists „a) venituri din activități
  independente, definite conform art. 67;” with no CIT carve-out, and **art. 68 alin. (1)** has a *closed*
  exception list — „…cu excepția situațiilor în care sunt aplicabile prevederile **art. 68^1, 68^3 și 69**”
  (Titlul II is not among it; art. 68 alin. (1) last amended by Legea 239/2025 Art. XII pct. 4,
  MO 1160/15.12.2025, eff. 01.01.2026).
- **The former option vehicle, art. 120 CF, is now „Abrogat”** — **OUG nr. 128/2024, Pct. 11, Art. I,
  MO nr. 1125 din 11.11.2024, eff. 01.01.2025.** Before 2025 a persoană fizică *could* opt into profit
  tax; for the **2026 income year it cannot**.
- Two corroborating negatives on the 08.08.2026 consolidation: a body-wide scan for „persoanele fizice
  care realizează venituri din activități independente” within ±260 chars of „impozit pe profit” /
  „titlului II” returned **0 hits**; art. 125 contains **zero** occurrences of „impozit pe profit” or
  „opțiune”.

**App consequence.** `impozit_pe_cit` is not an alternative Titlul IV base — it is a **legally impossible
combination for a PFA**. So the income-tax gate must compute for `impozit_pe_venit` and return
`REVIEW_REQUIRED` for `impozit_pe_cit`, but the latter must be framed as a **data-integrity failure**
(wrong regime recorded, or the entity is actually a persoană juridică) — **not** as "a different base
applies". It also means the `PfaRegime` union itself offers a choice that cannot exist: see **B-14** and
the Phase 6 note in `PLAN.md`.

**Confidence MEDIUM-HIGH.** Evidence: art. 14 lit. a) + art. 61 + art. 68 alin. (1) closed list +
abrogated art. 120 + two negative scans. What would raise it to HIGH: a positive read of Titlul II
Cap. I (art. 13–15) confirming no „persoană fizică” hook anywhere — **not done**.
