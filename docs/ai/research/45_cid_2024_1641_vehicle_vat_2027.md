# R-CID — CID (EU) 2024/1641 at 31 Dec 2026: what is in force, and what the 50 % rule actually rests on

**Researched by** `researcher-cid-1641-status` on 2026-10-12/13, web only. **Written to file by the orchestrator** — the Researcher has no filesystem access (§12.16, §12.14). **Answers B-94.**

**Every "as of" statement below is dated 13 October 2026 (UTC, verified via browser clock).** The project date is 12 October 2026. **This is a freshness check, and it must be re-run** — see §Re-check triggers.

**Headline.** The derogation is **in force and ends 31 December 2026**; **no extension has been adopted**, only proposed. **But the decisive finding reverses what our own corpus asserted: the 50 % limit is carried by art. 298 alin. (1) Cod fiscal, which is a free-standing domestic rule — no date, no sunset, no reference to the EU derogation at all.** So it does **not** silently expire on 1 January 2027. **`research/42` said the opposite, in those words, and is corrected below.**

## 1. Status of the derogation — FACT

**1.1 In force, end date 31 December 2026.** EUR-Lex record for CELEX **32024D1641** (checked 13 Oct 2026): status **„In force"**; OJ L, 2024/1641, 6.6.2024; **„Date of end of validity: 31/12/2026; See Art. 5.2"**. Derogation relations recorded against **art. 168** and **art. 26(1)(a)** of Directive 2006/112/EC, both for 01/01/2024–31/12/2026. [EUR-Lex 32024D1641](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX:32024D1641)

**1.2 The article that sets it — Art. 5(2), verbatim:**
> „1. This Decision shall take effect on the date of its notification. 2. **This Decision shall apply from 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2026.** 3. Any request for an extension of the special measures laid down in Articles 1 and 3 shall be submitted the Commission by 31 March 2026 and shall be accompanied by a report which includes a review of the percentage limitation applied on the right to deduct VAT on the basis of this Decision."

**Art. 1** is the operative restriction — „By way of derogation from Article 168 … Romania is authorised to limit to 50 % the right to deduct the VAT on the purchase, intra-Community acquisition, importation, hire or leasing of motorised road vehicles as well as the VAT charged on expenditure related to those vehicles, where the vehicle is not used exclusively for business purposes." **Art. 3** is the paired derogation from **art. 26(1)(a)** — no deemed supply for private use.

**1.3 ⚠️ Two citation errors in our register — corrected.**
- The CELEX is **32024D1641**, **not** 32024R1641. „R" is for regulations; this is a non-legislative **Decision**.
- The derogation is from **art. 26(1)(a) and art. 168** of Directive 2006/112/EC — **not „art. 168 and 169"**. Art. 169 (extension of deduction to cross-border/exempt transactions) is untouched. Legal basis: art. 395(1) first subparagraph.

**1.4 Authorisation lineage — FACT** (from the Commission's explanatory memorandum, 10 Sept 2026): Decision **2012/232/EU** → 31 Dec 2014; CID **2015/156** → 31 Dec 2017; CID **2017/2012** → 31 Dec 2020; CID **2020/1262** → 31 Dec 2023; CID **2024/1641** → 31 Dec 2026. Each renewal „a new but, as to the content, identical derogation". [ST-13267-2026-INIT](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-13267-2026-INIT/en/pdf)

## 3. What applies the day after, if nothing replaces it

- **FACT — art. 168 becomes operative unqualified:** deduction follows **actual use for taxed transactions**. No flat percentage is available.
- **FACT — correction to a common framing: art. 168a is not a motor-vehicle provision.** Art. 168a(1) concerns **immovable property**; art. 168a(2) lets Member States *extend* that proportionate approach to other business assets **if they choose**. **It does not authorise a flat 50 % cut-off.**
- **FACT — art. 169 is not the basis either.** On lapse the chain is **art. 168 + art. 173–175 (pro rata) + art. 184–192 (adjustments)**.
- **FACT — art. 26(1)(a) springs back:** private use of business assets becomes a **deemed taxable supply** again. CID Art. 3 is what currently disapplies it for 50 %-restricted cars.
- **INFERENCE (well-supported) — there is no „standstill" fallback for motor vehicles.** The consolidated Directive contains **no occurrence of „motor vehicle"** and no reference to 1 January 1991; the Sixth Directive's grandfathering clause maps to **Art. 394**, which grandfathers only measures applied **at 1 January 1977** and notified by 1 January 1978 — **unusable for a 2007 accession state.** That is why Romania has needed an art. 395 derogation continuously since 2012/232/EU. **On lapse, the only lawful route to a flat 50 % is a fresh art. 395 authorisation.**
- **Practical meaning (INFERENCE):** deduction would track real business use, evidenced by *foi de parcurs*, with pro rata where mixed with exempt activity, adjustment of previously deducted capital-goods VAT (Romanian art. 305, 5-year monitoring), **and** output VAT on the private-use share as a deemed supply. **The opposite of today's simplification**, which the Commission describes as removing „the need for the taxable person to keep records of mileage relating to business use and mileage relating to private use".

## 4. The domestic anchor — the question that decides everything

**4.1 FACT — the 50 % limit is art. 298 alin. (1) Cod fiscal, not art. 297.** Art. 297 is *„Sfera de aplicare a dreptului de deducere"* — the general scope article **that art. 298 derogates from**. Art. 298 is *„Limitări speciale ale dreptului de deducere"*. Verbatim:
> **Art. 298 alin. (1):** „Prin excepție de la prevederile art. 297 se limitează la 50% dreptul de deducere a taxei aferente cumpărării, achiziției intracomunitare, importului, închirierii sau leasingului de vehicule rutiere motorizate și a taxei aferente cheltuielilor legate de vehiculele aflate în proprietatea sau în folosința persoanei impozabile, în cazul în care vehiculele nu sunt utilizate exclusiv în scopul activității economice."

alin. (2) — >3.500 kg or >9 seats; alin. (3) lit. a)–f) — the six excluded categories; alin. (4) — exclusively-business and excluded vehicles fall back to art. 297 and 299–301; alin. (5) — normele metodologice. *(Our app already cites art. 298 correctly — the art. 297 error was in prose, not in code.)*

**4.2 FACT — the key answer: art. 298 alin. (1) is a standalone domestic rule.** **No date, no time limit, no sunset clause, no reference to Directive 2006/112/EC, to a Council implementing decision, or to Romania being „autorizată".** Its only condition is factual — whether the vehicle is used exclusively for the economic activity. **Nothing in it is contingent on CID 2024/1641 remaining in force.**

**4.3 CORROBORATED (strong) — the legislator knows how to make a rule derogation-dependent, and did so two alineate away.** OUG 115/2023 art. LIII pct. 116 cor. art. LVII alin. (1) lit. b) inserted **art. 298 alin. (4^1)** (50 % for buildings/residential premises) with an express conditional commencement: „**Începând cu data de 1 a lunii următoare datei de la care România este autorizată să aplice o măsură specială de derogare de la dispozițiile Directivei 2006/112/CE…**". **Conditional for buildings, unconditional for cars.** That asymmetry is the best available evidence that alin. (1) does not self-terminate. *(Our `research/42` already records alin. (4^1) as still not in force — it corroborates this without the two being reconciled until now.)*

**4.4 FACT — the domestic rule predates the derogation chain.** Introduced by **OUG 34/2009** as art. 145¹ of Legea 571/2003 (1 May 2009–31 Dec 2010, initially *full* non-deduction), took its **50 % flat form** as amended (norms at pct. 45¹, HG 670/2012, from 1 July 2012), carried into art. 298 alin. (1) of the 2015 Cod fiscal with norms at **pct. 68 HG 1/2016**.

**4.5 FACT — the operative norm-level rule is pct. 68 alin. (4) HG 1/2016**, which is what makes the 50 % an *irrebuttable simplification*: „…se limitează la 50% dreptul de deducere … **Persoana impozabilă care aplică deducerea limitată de 50% nu trebuie să facă dovada utilizării vehiculului … cu ajutorul foii de parcurs.**" Pct. 68 alin. (2) is the mirror rule for 100 % with foi de parcurs.

**4.6 INFERENCE (high confidence) — the answer for the app.** The rule **does not silently become wrong on 1 January 2027 as a matter of Romanian positive law.** If the derogation lapses unreplaced:
- art. 298 alin. (1) **stays on the statute book unchanged**; repealing it takes a legislative act (CF art. 4);
- but it would be **contrary to art. 168 VATD**, with **no standstill fallback** (§3), EU law prevailing;
- a taxpayer refusing the cap and claiming use-based deduction would have a **directly-effective EU-law argument**, while ANAF would in practice keep applying the Cod fiscal until amended.
- **So FY2027 exposure is legal-conflict risk, not automatic rule change — and the risk runs the way that the taxpayer's entitlement could be *broader* than the app computes, not narrower.**

## 6. Verdict for the app
1. **FY2026 — keep the 50 %.** Both legs hold. No change.
2. **FY2027 — do not change the rule; flag it.** Switching to 100 % on 1 Jan 2027 would be **wrong under Romanian law as it stands.**
3. **Attach a dated status flag:** *50 % limit under art. 298 alin. (1) CF; EU authorisation CID (EU) 2024/1641 expires 31 Dec 2026; extension to 31 Dec 2029 proposed 10 Sept 2026 (COM(2026) 457 / 2026/0253(NLE)) and **not adopted as of 13 Oct 2026**.*
4. **Model the tail risk, not the headline:** on lapse the 50 % remains the *de facto* ANAF position but becomes challengeable under art. 168, **and** private use becomes a deemed supply under art. 26(1)(a) — a new computation the app does not have.
5. **Re-check triggers:** (a) when the Council adopts — watch EUR-Lex 32024D1641 „Subsequent related instruments" and the OJ; (b) **again in the last week of December 2026**, to confirm whether adoption landed before the lapse.

## 7. Unresolved
1. **Adoption date.** Nothing scheduled publicly; art. 395 derogations are often adopted by written procedure / „A point" without appearing on an ECOFIN agenda — **absence from agendas is not proof of delay.** EUR-Lex is the tripwire.
2. **Retroactivity if adoption slips past 31 Dec 2026.** The draft takes effect „on the date of its notification" and, unlike CID 2024/1641 (recital 7, which backdated to 1 Jan 2024 on legitimate-expectations grounds), **carries no equivalent recital for a 2027 gap.** Real but unquantified.
3. **Romania's legislative fallback** — no draft OUG/bill amending art. 298 found. Repeal, redraft (an art. 168a(2)-style proportionate rule), or leave an incompatible rule in place: unknown.
4. **The Romanian transposition of art. 26(1)(a)** deemed supply was not verified — needed only if the app models the post-lapse scenario.
5. **Official consolidated art. 298** not extractable from legislatie.just.ro (truncation).
6. **art. 298 alin. (4^1)** (buildings) **is** expressly derogation-conditional and has its own pending authorisation — **a distinct staleness risk adjacent to this one.**
