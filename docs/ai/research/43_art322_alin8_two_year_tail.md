# R-A8 — CF art. 322 alin. (8): the two-year monthly tail, and how one gets out of it

**Researched by** `researcher-art322-alin8` on 2026-10-12, web only. **Written to file by the orchestrator** — the Researcher has no filesystem access (§12.14). **Closes the gap found while preparing B-102.**

**Question:** our `research/33` §7 quote of art. 322 alin. (8) is elided at both ends, and the trailing ellipsis removes the clause that decides how the two-year monthly obligation ends. Full text, the effective date, and whether the tail depends on the 5-working-day declaration.

**Headline.** Alin. (8) is **three sentences**, and the third — missing from our file — is the hinge: **„În acest sens va trebui să depună declarația de mențiuni prevăzută la alin. (6)."** The move **to** the month is created by alin. (7) and is **not** conditioned on any filing; the move **back** to the quarter **is** conditioned — on the alin. (2) turnover cap, on there being no intra-community acquisition in the following year, and on a declaration actually being filed. **The statute states no effective date for the return.**

---

## 1. Art. 322 alin. (8), FY2026, verbatim, nothing elided — **FACT**

legislatie.just.ro consolidated Cod fiscal, forma consolidată la **08.08.2026** ([doc 312600](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/312600), [text 312601](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/312601)); Legea nr. 227/2015, MO 688/10.09.2015. **Identical in the 01.01.2026 consolidation** ([text 305351](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/305351)) — so nothing changed during FY2026.

> **(8)** Persoana impozabilă **care potrivit alin. (7) este obligată să își schimbe perioada fiscală** trebuie să depună o declarație de mențiuni **la organul fiscal competent**, în termen de maximum 5 zile lucrătoare de la finele lunii în care intervine exigibilitatea achiziției intracomunitare **care generează această obligație**, și va utiliza ca perioadă fiscală luna calendaristică pentru anul curent și pentru anul următor. Dacă în cursul anului următor nu efectuează nicio achiziție intracomunitară de bunuri, persoana respectivă va reveni conform alin. (1) la trimestrul calendaristic **drept perioadă fiscală**. **În acest sens va trebui să depună declarația de mențiuni prevăzută la alin. (6).**

- **Antecedent:** the subject is defined *by reference to alin. (7)* — „persoana impozabilă care utilizează trimestrul calendaristic ca perioadă fiscală și care efectuează o achiziție intracomunitară de bunuri taxabilă în România". Not a free-standing clause. **The app needs exactly this link.**
- **Three sentences.** ANAF itself calls the second one **„alin. (8) teza a II-a"**.
- **No amendment note on alin. (8) — CONFIRMED.** A machine scan of the whole art. 322 block in **both** consolidations returned **zero** inline amendment notes, for alin. (8) and every other alineat, while such notes appear immediately before and after art. 322 in the same file. `research/33` §7's finding stands. *(Provenance CORROBORATED: the mechanism predates 2015 — the 092 form existed in 2009 under art. 156¹ alin. (6¹)–(6²) of Legea 571/2003, [OPANAF 1165/2009 anexa](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/OPANAF_1165_2009.pdf).)*

## 2. „conform alin. (1) la trimestrul calendaristic" — the text really does say that — **FACT (text)**

Verbatim: *„…va reveni conform alin. (1) la trimestrul calendaristic drept perioadă fiscală."* Our transcription was right; it only dropped the final three words. **Alin. (1) is the month rule**, so the cross-reference is incoherent on its face. **This is a drafting defect in the law, not an error in our file.**

**What the return is actually conditioned on — FACT from ANAF's own instruments:**

- **OPANAF 631/2016**, instructions for formular 094: the form is also filed by those in „art. 322 alin. (8) **teza a II-a**" — who return to the quarter „întrucât în anul precedent/anii precedenți au utilizat luna ca perioadă fiscală, dar nu au mai efectuat achiziții intracomunitare de bunuri **și nu au depășit plafonul de 100.000 euro**" ([Dec_094_OPANAF_631_2016.pdf](http://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/formulare/Dec_094_OPANAF_631_2016.pdf)).
- **Current FY2026 registration forms** (OPANAF 1699/2021 as amended by **OPANAF 15/2026**, MO 17/14.01.2026) carry a pre-printed declaration: *„În temeiul dispozițiilor art. 322 alin. (8) teza a II-a … **revin la trimestrul calendaristic** drept perioadă fiscală, întrucât … nu am mai efectuat achiziții intracomunitare de bunuri **și cifra de afaceri nu a depășit plafonul prevăzut la art. 322 alin. (2)**"* ([D_013](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/formulare/D_013_OPANAF_15_2026.pdf), [D_016](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/formulare/D_016_OPANAF_15_2026.pdf), [D_020](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/formulare/D_020_OPANAF_15_2026.pdf), [D_070](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/formulare/D_070_OPANAF_15_2026.pdf)).
- **ANAF administrative practice says it outright** — AJFP Galați, 12.01.2026: „Dacă în cursul anului următor nu efectuează nicio achiziție intracomunitară de bunuri **și realizează o cifră de afaceri care nu depășește plafonul de 100.000 euro**, persoana respectivă va reveni la trimestrul calendaristic drept perioadă fiscală" ([ajfp_gl_tva.pdf](https://static.anaf.ro/static/3/Galati/20260112143418_ajfp_gl_tva.pdf)).

**Read `conform alin. (1)` as „back into the default-period regime of alin. (1)–(2)": the month stays the default, and the quarter applies only if alin. (2) is satisfied.** **INFERENCE**, well-supported: the literal alin. (1) cannot be the operative test, because alin. (1) prescribes the month.

## 3. When does the return take effect? **The statute never says.**

**FACT:** alin. (8) contains **no effective-date clause**. The clause our ellipsis removed is the **third sentence** — the alin. (6) declaration. There is no „începând cu primul trimestru al anului următor" anywhere in alin. (8); **that formulation is not in the law.**

**Where the date actually comes from — CORROBORATED:**
- Alin. (6) is a **year-level** mechanism: filed „până la data de 25 ianuarie inclusiv", stating the previous year's turnover and the no-ICAB statement ([D_015_OPANAF_15_2026.pdf](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/formulare/D_015_OPANAF_15_2026.pdf)).
- The 010-family instructions frame the switch year-wise: mark „trimestrială" if „în cursul anului calendaristic precedent" turnover stayed under the cap.
- **Worked example** (ICAB in March 2019): monthly for **2019 and 2020**, change „**pentru anul 2021**", filings due 15.01.2021 (formular 700, art. 88 alin. (1) CPF) and 25.01.2021 (formular 094) — [InfoTVA case study](https://infotva.manager.ro/articole/studii-de-caz/revenire-la-trimestru-ca-perioada-fiscala-de-raportare-tva-formulare-necesare-si-termen-limita-21076.html).
- ANAF's internal procedure updates the register „din lună calendaristică în trimestru calendaristic" on the basis of the 094 (pct. 8.4) **without stating a date** ([Procedura 30/01/2019](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/247180)).

**INFERENCE (supported, not statutory):** the return operates for the **whole calendar year following the second monthly year** — **year N+2** where the triggering acquisition was in year **N**. **If the app needs a hard date rule, it is an interpretation and must be labelled as one.**

## 4. Is the two-year tail conditioned on the 5-working-day declaration? **No.** — **FACT (text) + CORROBORATED**

- **Text structure:** the period change is created by alin. (7) („perioada fiscală **devine** luna calendaristică începând cu…"), and alin. (8)'s subject is defined by it. Alin. (8) then adds two things **in parallel**: a filing duty, and the two-year rule („**și va utiliza**…"). **The filing is not framed as a condition precedent.**
- **Sanction, not forfeiture:** Procedura 30/01/2019 pct. 9 — filing the 092 late „**constituie contravenție** potrivit art. 336 alin. (1) lit. a) din Legea nr. 207/2015". **A missed filing produces a fine, not a cancelled tail.**
- **ANAF applies the tail with no filing at all:** pct. 10–16 — ANAF compiles a quarterly list from the 300 returns, notifies taxpayers who declared an ICAB but never filed the 092, and updates the register **din oficiu**, anchoring the take-on to „data de 1 a primei luni a trimestrului pentru care a fost depus decontul de TVA în care a fost declarată achiziția intracomunitară". Pct. 6 anchors to **data exigibilității**, not the filing date.

### The asymmetry — the single most important finding for B-102

> **The switch *to* the month is fact-driven and ANAF enforces it ex officio. The return *to* the quarter is not self-executing: it requires the alin. (6) mențiuni filing, and without it the register stays monthly.**

**An app that auto-returns a taxpayer to quarterly after two years would be wrong in exactly the case the law cares about.**

## 5. The two mențiuni declarations are genuinely distinct — **FACT**

| | alin. (8) teza I | alin. (6) |
|---|---|---|
| Basis | art. 322 alin. (8) teza I | art. 322 alin. (6) |
| Trigger | on the quarter **and** effecting an ICAB taxabilă în România (alin. (7)) | obliged to file quarterly „potrivit alin. (2) și (5)" |
| Content | declares the change of period (month + year of exigibilitatea; the 1st-of-month date from which the month applies) | „cifra de afaceri din anul precedent **și** o mențiune referitoare la faptul că nu a efectuat achiziții intracomunitare de bunuri în anul precedent" |
| Deadline | „maximum 5 zile lucrătoare de la finele lunii în care intervine exigibilitatea…" | „până la data de 25 ianuarie inclusiv" (ANAF: „pentru anul precedent") |
| Historical form | **092** (Procedura 30/01/2019 anexa nr. 2; orig. OPANAF 1165/2009) | **094** (OPANAF 631/2016 anexa nr. 3) |
| **FY2026 vehicle** | **010-family / 700, Subsecțiunea II rândul 1.12** (1.6 / 1.8 / 1.18 depending on form) | **rândul 1.13** (1.7 / 1.9 / 1.19), with **1.13.2** reserved for „art. 322 alin. (8) teza a II-a" |

- **The Cod fiscal names no form for either.** A form-number citation must cite the **ANAF order**, never the CF.
- **092/094 are safe as historical / ANAF-procedure citations, not as FY2026 form identifiers.** **Checked against our tree: the app cites neither number, so no defect exists here.**
- **The link between them is the sentence our quote dropped:** alin. (8) sends the taxpayer back via „declarația de mențiuni **prevăzută la alin. (6)**", and ANAF implements exactly that (pct. 8.3).
- **Our model is correct:** `aicMentiuniObligation` keyed to alin. (8) (5 working days from month-end) and a separate alin. (6) obligation (25 January) are both right and distinct.

## Correction to `research/33` §7

The alin. (8) quote there was **imprecise — replaced 2026-10-12**. Four defects: (1) **missing leading clause** — it began mid-sentence at „trebuie să depună", losing the alin. (7) antecedent the app needs; (2) ellipsis hid **„la organul fiscal competent"**; (3) ellipsis hid **„care generează această obligație"**; (4) **the material one** — it ended at „…la trimestrul calendaristic…", dropping **„drept perioadă fiscală"** and the whole **third sentence**, which is the hinge between the two-year tail and the 25 January declaration. **§7's finding that art. 322 carries no amendment note on any alineat is confirmed.**

## Gaps that remain

1. **No statutory effective date for the return.** „1 January of year N+2" is a **supported inference** from the alin. (6) mechanism and ANAF practice, not a quoted clause.
2. **Status of Procedura 30/01/2019 in FY2026.** Still published as „actualizată", still names the 092, while the FY2026 route is the 010/700 family. No abrogating act found; not confirmed unamended either.
3. **Does a missed alin. (6) filing permanently forfeit the return, or does a late filing still effect a retroactive return?** No authority found. **This is the mirror of §4 and remains open.** It does **not** block B-102: defaulting to *stays monthly until a filing is recorded* matches ANAF's own behaviour (pct. 8.4) and is the safe side of the uncertainty.
4. **Deeper legislative history of alin. (8)** — out of scope; irrelevant to FY2026, since the 2015 republication is the base text and no note attaches to art. 322.
