# R-C — FY2027 sunsets and whether carrying 2026 values into 2027 is safe

**Researcher:** `scout-R-c-fy2027-sunsets`, 2026-10-10. Transcribed by the Orchestrator — the `researcher` profile has no write tool (**Q-RESEARCHER-WRITE**), so this file is the record of record.

**Question:** what is already known about fiscal changes taking effect 1 January 2027, and what sunsets on 31 December 2026 — so that "carry the last known values forward" (**D-YEAR-2**) can be decided with the failure modes named.

**Why it mattered:** D-YEAR-1 auto-switches the fiscal year on 1 January and D-YEAR-2 says carry the last known values forward until the act lands. Neither had ever been checked against what actually changes at that boundary.

In re-verifying it the researcher **found six items in the lost draft wrong or unverified and corrected them**, each marked ⚠️ CORRECTED below. ## Headline

No HG fixing the minimum wage for 1 ianuarie 2027 exists as of 9–10 October 2026, and no 2027 budget law or 2027 fiscal act exists — the interim cabinet is constitutionally barred from tabling one.

**Carrying 2026 values into 2027 is safe for the statutory calendar and for the VAT plafon, and unsafe for every SM-derived figure.**

⚠️ **The single most important correction: the correct FY2027 carry-forward seed is 4.325 lei, not 4.050 lei.** Under art. 135¹ alin. (3) the governing value is „în vigoare la data de 1 ianuarie 2027", which is the value set by **HG 146/2026** (applicable from 1 iulie 2026, no later act). An app that carries 4.050 into 2027 is wrong **even if the wage is frozen**.

The sunset register is larger than the three already known: **18 date-limited Cod fiscal provisions end on 31 Decembrie 2026**, while the consolidated **Cod de procedură fiscală contains zero** — its only 2026 sunset arrives from outside the code, via OUG 13/2026 art. V.

## P1 — The minimum wage for 1 ianuarie 2027

### Status: not published (DIRECT)

The governing acts remain:

**HG 146/2026** (12 martie 2026), **MO 196/13.03.2026** — Portal Legislativ **308231**. Art. 1 verbatim:
> „Începând cu data de 1 iulie 2026, salariul de bază minim brut pe țară garantat în plată, prevăzut la art. 164 alin. (1) din Legea nr. 53/2003 - Codul muncii, republicată, cu modificările și completările ulterioare, se stabilește în bani, fără a include sporuri și alte adaosuri, la suma de **4.325 lei lunar**, pentru un program normal de lucru în medie de 166,667 ore pe lună, reprezentând 25,949 lei/oră."

Art. 2 verbatim: „La data prevăzută la art. 1, Hotărârea Guvernului nr. 1.506/2024 … publicată în Monitorul Oficial al României, Partea I, nr. 1185 din 28 noiembrie 2024, **se abrogă**."

**HG 1.506/2024** (27 noiembrie 2024), **MO 1185/28.11.2024**, PL **291450**. Art. 1: „Începând cu data de 1 ianuarie 2025, … la suma de **4.050 lei lunar**, pentru un program normal de lucru în medie de 165,334 ore pe lună, reprezentând 24,496 lei/oră." — this is the value „în vigoare la data de 1 ianuarie 2026".

### ⚠️ CORRECTED — the FY2027 seed is 4.325 lei, not 4.050 lei

The lost draft said to seed FY2027 at 4.050. **Wrong.** CF art. 135¹ alin. (3) fixes the governing value as „valoarea salariului minim brut pe țară stabilit prin hotărâre a Guvernului, **în vigoare la data de 1 ianuarie a anului de realizare a venitului**". On 1 ianuarie 2027, absent a new HG, the HG in force is HG 146/2026 and the amount it fixes is **4.325 lei**.

| | SM | 6 SM | 12 SM | 24 SM | 72 SM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FY2026** (HG 1.506/2024) | 4.050 | 24.300 | 48.600 | 97.200 | 291.600 |
| **FY2027 carried** (HG 146/2026) | **4.325** | **25.950** | **51.900** | **103.800** | **311.400** |

**Carrying 4.050 into 2027 is a two-step-stale value and is wrong even under a wage freeze.**

### The procedural calendar — HG 35/2025 (DIRECT, verbatim)

HG 35/2025 (6 februarie 2025), **MO 111/06.02.2025**. Art. 4 alin. (5): „Pentru stabilirea indicatorilor prognozați se vor utiliza indicatorii din prognoza interimară de toamnă a Comisiei Naționale de Strategie și Prognoză, care va fi pusă la dispoziția Ministerului Muncii … **până la data de 10 octombrie** a anului curent." Art. 5 alin. (1): „Salariul de bază minim brut pe țară garantat în plată se stabilește și se actualizează anual cu suma dintre rata medie anuală a inflației prognozate și rata de creștere reală anuală a productivității muncii prognozate." Art. 3 lit. c) sets the orientative benchmark at „nivelul orientativ de 47%-52%" of the SM in the average gross wage.

**Labelled as reported, not quoted law:** the „20 octombrie" report deadline is **not** a text string in HG 35/2025. It comes from the interim minister's public statement.

### The 2027 calendar has already slipped (CORROBORATED, secondary)

- Ministrul interimar Dragoș Pîslaru, 25 Aug 2026: „Nicio decizie privind salariul minim 2027 nu poate fi luată în acest moment", and „Termenul, după cum știți, conform hotărârii de Guvern, este de 20 octombrie…". He stressed that setting SM „este o prerogativă care nu poate fi exercitată decât de un Guvern cu puteri depline, nu de unul interimar".
- **Până la 28 septembrie 2026 nu exista un proiect de hotărâre pentru salariul minim din 2027.**
- Sindicatele cer **5.000 lei** brut de la 1 ianuarie 2027; patronatele argumentează menţinerea lui 4.325; PM Bolojan: „până la Sărbătoarea Crăciunului, va fi luată o decizie". **A freeze is a live scenario.**
- The 2026 calendar was itself not followed: SM stayed 4.050 for the first six months and rose from 1 iulie by an HG adopted 12 martie 2026.

**INFERRED, practical read:** a 1-January wage act has historically landed **October–December** of the preceding year, with one precedent of a **January** act effective on publication. And there is a direct precedent for pure carry-forward: **1 ianuarie 2026 had no wage act at all** — the governing value was set 13 months earlier. The app must run on a carried value for at least 4–8 weeks into January, and in the worst case (freeze, or a January act) for the whole year.

### Unsafe to carry forward — the SM multiples, and the direction of error is not uniform

| Figure | FY2027 carried (SM 4.325) | Direction if the true SM is higher |
|---|---|---|
| **6 SM** — art. 174 alin. (6) top-up floor | 25.950 | **UNDERSTATES** the top-up — unsafe |
| **12 SM** — art. 148 alin. (2) CAS minimum base; art. 69 alin. (3) normă floor | 51.900 | **MIXED.** For net income between the carried and the true 12 SM the app says **CAS is due when it may not be → OVERSTATES**. For the normă floor: **UNDERSTATES**. |
| **24 SM** — art. 170 alin. (3) CASS tier boundary | 103.800 | **MIXED / income-dependent.** Worked example at 110.000 lei: carried → 24 SM tier, base 103.800; at SM 4.600 the 24 SM level is 110.400, so the income falls in the 12 SM tier with base 55.200 → carried value **OVERSTATES**. At other income levels it understates. |
| **72 SM** — art. 170 alin. (1) CASS ceiling | 311.400 | **UNDERSTATES** CASS for high earners — the app caps the base too early |
| **Normă de venit** | FY2026 county decisions | **UNKNOWN — the 2027 dataset does not exist yet.** Carrying is safe only if the county re-publishes an identical figure, which is not guaranteed |
| **25.000 euro gate** | EUR-anchored | **Not carried** — recomputed from the BNR annual average *for the income year*. Safe by construction |

**This is the core design finding.** The same stale wage simultaneously *overstates* CAS in the band between the carried and true 12 SM, *overstates* CASS through the tier-boundary effect at some income levels, *understates* the art. 174 alin. (6) top-up, and *understates* CASS through the 72 SM cap at high incomes. **There is no single conservative direction.** A "safe" carry-forward would have to be per-figure and per-income-band — which is why the honest design is a **provisional, visibly-flagged value with a recompute trigger**, not a deliberately padded one.

## Orchestrator notes — the local half the researcher could not do

R-C's gap 9 said local evidence is outside its boundary: which figures the app stores, and where carry-forward happens. I checked in-tree on `master`. **The result changes what the finding is.**

**1. There is no silent 2026→2027 carry-forward, and the app refuses rather than guesses.** `selectRuleSet(taxYear, asOfDate)` (`src/domain/fiscal/registry.ts:378`) returns `undefined` for any year with no registered release, and only 2026 is registered (`release2026.ts`, `package2026.ts`). All three call sites map that to an empty rule set — `src/data/derived.ts:44`, `src/data/dashboard.ts:62`, `src/pages/Expenses.tsx:24` — and `derived.ts:42-43` states the intent in a comment: *"a profile that says another year gets no rules instead of getting 2026's."* The engine then refuses with a named reason: `src/domain/tax.ts:138-140` returns `review_required` with „Regula CAS nu a fost găsită pentru anul fiscal 2027" and its CASS and income-tax siblings. **No wrong number is produced.** That is the design holding, and it should be said plainly rather than assumed.

**2. But that makes D-YEAR-1 and D-YEAR-2 collide, and that collision is a defect — B-58.** D-YEAR-1 auto-switches the fiscal year on 1 January. On 1 ianuarie 2027 the app will switch to a year for which **no release exists**, and every figure goes to `review_required`: the hero, the thresholds (which `continue` past any spec whose rule is absent — `thresholds.ts:783-785`, so the rows simply disappear), the deadlines and the reserve. D-YEAR-2's "carry the last known values forward" has no mechanism to carry into. The app is honest and useless at the same moment, and the trigger is its own.

**3. The thresholds are stored as lei, with the SM multiple kept only as a cross-check — B-59.** `THRESHOLD_SPECS` carry `statutorySmMultiplier`, and `computedLeiFor` (`thresholds.ts:747-748`) verifies `smRef.valueBani × multiplier === thresholdValue`, dropping the label when it does not hold. So a mismatch is caught — but as a **presentation label and a failing test**, not as a computation guard. The operative figure is the lei parameter in the year's package (`thresholds.ts:786-789` reads `rule.parameters`), while the law expresses these thresholds as multiples of SM. Building 2027 means hand-writing six new lei literals that must agree with a new SM rule, and the only thing enforcing the agreement is a test.

**4. Two of R-C's "must not carry" items are already handled, and one is out of scope.** The **bonificație** was never encoded: `package2026.ts:269-272` cites OUG 8/2026 art. 8 expressly to say *do not* encode 15 April 2026 as a deadline, calling it "the single most likely source of a recurrence of that error". The **395.000-lei VAT plafon** is modelled with no SM multiplier at all — `thresholds.ts:259` records that "the lei figure is the statute's own number", which independently corroborates R-C's "standing value, safe to carry". **TVA la încasare and taxare inversă are not modelled anywhere in `src`** — art. 282 appears only as a note in `release2026.ts:101` that Legea 239/2025 pct. 38 amends art. 282 alin. (3) and does *not* touch art. 310. So the art. 282 and art. 331 sunsets are **scope notes, not defects**: nothing exists to carry them wrongly.

**5. The 4.325 trap is sharper in-tree than in the report.** `package2026.ts` already contains **both** values — `lei(4050)` as `GENERAL_MINIMUM_WAGE_2026_H1` and `lei(4325)` as `…_H2`. So the correct FY2027 anchor is present in the codebase but labelled as an H2 **2026** period value. Anyone building a 2027 package by copying the 2026 one will find 4.050 first, in the first rule, with the right answer sitting below it under a year-specific name.

**6. One citation we did not have.** The norme de venit nomenclator is **OMFP 925/2017 as amended by OMF 1960/2025, MO 1215/30.12.2025**. `research/30` cited OMFP 925/2017 without the amending order. This matters for 12H: the annex shape R-B2 measured (128 positions nationally, 129 in the DGRFP Ploiești counties) is the shape of the **amended** order.

**7. What 12H now has, and does not have.** R-C gives 12H a **fetch window** — art. 69 alin. (2) lit. b) puts the 2027 decisions in Q4 2026, and the 2027 URLs are 404 today — and a **floor that auto-updates**: art. 69 alin. (3) makes no 2027 normă valid below 12 × SM(1 ianuarie 2027), so the dataset cannot be validated until the wage HG lands. That is a dependency between 12H and the SM watch that neither R-B nor R-B2 identified.
