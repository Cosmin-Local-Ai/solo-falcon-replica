# Research 22 — Deductibility caps under CF art. 68 and their conversion (FY2026, PFA în sistem real)

Captured 2026-10-09 by the Researcher (`research-caps-2c`). Primary sources only. Verbatim Romanian preserved.

## Headline

The euro-denominated caps in art. 68 alin. (5) Cod fiscal are **not** converted by any rule inside art. 68 itself — but they **are** fixed by the norms: **HG 1/2016, Normele metodologice, Titlul IV, Cap. II, Secțiunea a 2-a, pct. 7 alin. (6)** converts them at **„cursul mediu comunicat de Banca Națională a României pentru anul în care s-a efectuat plata"** (BNR **annual average** rate for the **year of payment**). Art. 9 alin. (3)–(4) CF is the general fallback (BNR rate **at the date of payment**), and it defers only to dates „prevăzute expres în prezentul cod" — so the norm's average-rate rule and the Code's own general rule point to **different rates**, and no primary text reconciles them. Everything else (per-person/per-year delimitation, the 5 % contribuții profesionale base, the protocol base and its two-pass mechanics) is settled by the text; the **selection of which car** takes the „un singur autoturism" cap when a taxpayer has two or more is **not settled by any primary source**.

**Scope of sources used.** Legea nr. 227/2015 (Codul fiscal), forma consolidată la **08.08.2026** (Portal Legislativ, doc. 312601) and la 31.03.2026 (doc. 306154); Normele metodologice aprobate prin HG nr. 1/2016, forma consolidată la **31.03.2026** (doc. 307847) and la 06.01.2016 (doc. 174823); OUG nr. 8/2026 (doc. 307580); ANAF's official annotated edition *Codul fiscal cu Normele metodologice* (`static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm`, self-declared currency: „ultima actualizare: LEGEA nr. 170/2026" / norms „Hotărârea Guvernului nr. 602/2025"). No blog, advisory or commercial guide is used as a source of record.

**Base publication data (verified):**
- Codul fiscal — Legea nr. 227/2015, **MONITORUL OFICIAL nr. 688 din 10 septembrie 2015**. The consolidated forms do not restate the Code's own entry-into-force date; only the rule for *modifications* appears (art. 4 alin. (1): „intră în vigoare în termen de minimum 6 luni de la publicare"; alin. (2): new/raised taxes and removed facilities enter into force on 1 January). **The Code's own entry-into-force date: UNVERIFIED from the texts read — do not hard-code it.**
- Normele metodologice — HG nr. 1 din 6 ianuarie 2016, **MONITORUL OFICIAL nr. 22 din 13 ianuarie 2016**. Art. 2 abrogates HG 44/2004 „la data intrării în vigoare a prezentei hotărâri", but no entry-into-force date is stated in the text read. **HG 1/2016 entry-into-force date: UNVERIFIED.**

## 1. The euro caps — which exchange rate, as of which date

### Answer

**Art. 68 CF itself fixes nothing.** None of lit. g), g^1), g^2), h), p), r), s) contains any conversion clause — they say only „în limita echivalentului în lei a 400 euro anual pentru fiecare persoană" (g, g^1, g^2, h, s), „în limita a 4.000 euro anual" (p), „în limita echivalentului în lei a 100 euro anual pentru fiecare persoană" (r). There is no alineat in art. 68 that names a rate or a date.

**HG 1/2016 does fix it — one single, generic norm.** Normele metodologice, Titlul IV „Impozitul pe venit", Capitolul II „Venituri din activități independente", Secțiunea a 2-a, **pct. 7 alin. (6)** converts *every* euro limit in art. 68 at the **BNR average rate for the year in which the payment was made**. This is the only euro-related norm anywhere in the norms for Titlul IV Cap. II: a full-text scan of the consolidated norms for that chapter (42,814 characters) returns exactly **one** occurrence of the string „euro", and it is this alineat.

**A general conversion rule also exists in the Code — art. 9 — and it points to a different date.** Art. 9 alin. (3) lit. b) converts at the rate „la data la care se primesc sau se plătesc sumele respective", and alin. (4) makes that the BNR rate „valabil pentru datele respective, **exceptând cazurile prevăzute expres în prezentul cod**". Art. 68 contains no such express carve-out, and the norms are not „prezentul cod". **The two operative rules therefore diverge: art. 9 → BNR rate on the payment date; pct. 7 alin. (6) → BNR annual average for the payment year.** No primary source read reconciles them. The app must not silently pick one.

For contrast, the Code *does* sometimes fix rates expressly (art. 69 alin. (9): „cursul de schimb mediu anual comunicat de Banca Națională a României, pentru anul de realizare a venitului"; art. 47 alin. (1) lit. c): „cel valabil la închiderea exercițiului financiar"). Nothing comparable exists for art. 68.

## 2. Is the euro cap per person and per year, and how is „anual" delimited?

### Answer

**Per year: yes, and it is the fiscal year, which for a PFA is the calendar year; the amounts are attributed by payment year.** Three texts interlock:
1. art. 65 alin. (1) CF — „Perioada impozabilă este anul fiscal care corespunde anului calendaristic."
2. art. 68 alin. (4) lit. b) CF — the expense must „să fie cuprinse în cheltuielile exercițiului financiar al anului **în cursul căruia au fost plătite**" — i.e. **payment year**, not accrual year.
3. pct. 7 alin. (6) Norme — the euro equivalent is struck at the BNR average „pentru **anul în care s-a efectuat plata**".

So „anual" = the calendar/fiscal year, and both the expense and the rate are pinned to the **year of payment**.

**Per person: only for some letters.** Lit. g), g^1), g^2), h), r), s) say „**pentru fiecare persoană**". Lit. p) says only „în limita a 4.000 euro **anual**" — **no per-person wording**. Lit. q) has no euro amount at all.

**Unresolved:** no primary source read states whether „pentru fiecare persoană" in lit. g)/h)/s) means one cap per *taxpayer* or one cap per *insured/covered person* (e.g. where the PFA pays health insurance or pension contributions for family members). Note the friction with art. 68 alin. (7) lit. a) CF, which makes „sumele sau bunurile utilizate de contribuabil pentru uzul personal **sau al familiei sale**" non-deductible, while lit. g)/h) expressly allow sums „plătite în scopul personal al contribuabilului".

### Verbatim Romanian

> „b) să fie cuprinse în cheltuielile exercițiului financiar al anului în cursul căruia au fost plătite;" — art. 68 alin. (4) lit. b) CF
> „(6) Limita echivalentului în euro a cheltuielilor deductibile limitat, prevăzută la art. 68 din Codul fiscal, se transformă în lei la cursul mediu comunicat de Banca Națională a României pentru anul în care s-a efectuat plata." — pct. 7 alin. (6) Norme

> „a) sumele sau bunurile utilizate de contribuabil pentru uzul personal sau al familiei sale;" — art. 68 alin. (7) lit. a) CF

## 3. Contribuții profesionale obligatorii — art. 68 alin. (5) lit. q)

### Answer
The cap is **5 % of „venitul brut realizat"**. „Venitul brut" is the same article's gross — defined by art. 68 alin. (2) (and negative-listed by alin. (3)), expanded by the norms at pct. 7 alin. (1), (1^1), (2). There is **no cross-reference to any other article's gross**, and **no „pentru fiecare persoană"** — the limit is a single 5 % of the gross realised, not a per-person allowance.

**Unresolved tension in the Code itself.** Art. 68 alin. (4) lit. i) lists, among the *generally deductible* (uncapped) conditions, „contribuții profesionale obligatorii plătite, potrivit legii, organizațiilor profesionale din care fac parte contribuabilii" — the same category that alin. (5) lit. q) caps at 5 %. **No norm in HG 1/2016 resolves which prevails**: the norms' illustrative list of deductible expenses (pct. 7 alin. (5)) does not mention contribuții profesionale at all, and the whole norms block for Titlul IV Cap. II contains no rule on the overlap.

### Verbatim Romanian
> „q) cheltuielile reprezentând contribuțiile profesionale obligatorii datorate, potrivit legii, organizațiilor profesionale din care fac parte contribuabilii, în limita a 5% din venitul brut realizat." — art. 68 alin. (5) lit. q) CF
> „i) să reprezinte contribuții de asigurări sociale plătite la sistemele proprii de asigurări sociale și/sau contribuții profesionale obligatorii plătite, potrivit legii, organizațiilor profesionale din care fac parte contribuabilii;" — art. 68 alin. (4) lit. i) CF
> „(1) Venitul net anual din activități independente se determină în sistem real, pe baza datelor din contabilitate, ca diferență între venitul brut și cheltuielile deductibile efectuate în scopul realizării de venituri, cu excepția situațiilor în care sunt aplicabile prevederile art. 68^1, 68^3 și 69." — art. 68 alin. (1) CF
> „(2) Venitul brut cuprinde: a) sumele încasate și echivalentul în lei al veniturilor în natură din desfășurarea activității; …" — art. 68 alin. (2) CF

## 4. Cheltuieli sociale — art. 68 alin. (5) lit. c), PFA fără salariați

### Answer
The base is **„valoarea anuală a cheltuielilor efectuate cu salariile personalului"**, and the destinations are those of art. 25 alin. (3) lit. b) Titlul II. With no personnel, that base is arithmetically zero, so the ceiling is zero. **No norm addresses the no-employee case.** A full-text scan of the consolidated norms for Titlul IV, Cap. II (42,814 characters, including the whole pct. 7 block on art. 68) returns **zero** occurrences of „cheltuielile sociale" or „cheltuieli sociale"; the occurrences of „sociale" in that chapter are unrelated („furnizori de servicii sociale", „contribuții sociale"). The only „cheltuielile sociale" norms in HG 1/2016 are in **Titlul II** (impozitul pe profit, art. 25 alin. (3) lit. b)), which is a different title and does not govern art. 68.

Note also that art. 68 alin. (4) lit. c) CF contemplates salary costs as a general condition, so the Code does not exclude a PFA from having personnel — it simply provides no rule for the zero base.

> „c) cheltuielile sociale, în limita unei cote de până la 5%, aplicată asupra valorii cheltuielilor cu salariile personalului, potrivit Codului muncii. Intră sub incidența acestei limite următoarele: 1. ajutoarele de înmormântare, ajutoarele pentru bolile grave și incurabile, ajutoarele pentru naștere, ajutoarele pentru proteze, ajutoarele pentru pierderi produse în gospodăriile proprii, aju…" — art. 25 alin. ## 5. One car per person — art. 68 alin. (5) lit. j) with alin. (7) lit. k)

### Answer
**How the vehicle is identified by the norms:**
- **Registrul-inventar** — pct. 7 alin. (8) Norme: all goods, rights and obligations relating to the activity are entered in the Registrul-inventar and constitute the business patrimony. This is the identification register.
- **Foaie de parcurs** — pct. 7 alin. (7) Norme: it is the instrument for proving **exclusive** business use and thereby obtaining **full** deductibility (i.e. escaping the 50 % cut in alin. (7) lit. k)); it must contain at least vehicle category, purpose and place of the trip, kilometres run, and own fuel-consumption norm per km.
- The meanings of „vehicule rutiere motorizate" / „utilizate exclusiv în scopul desfășurării activității" are borrowed from **pct. 68 of the norms given for art. 298 alin. (6) CF (TVA)**.

**What happens with two or more cars: UNVERIFIED.** Art. 68 alin. (5) lit. j) says the operating/maintenance/repair costs of „autoturismele folosite de contribuabil sau membru asociat" are limited „la **cel mult un singur autoturism aferent fiecărei persoane**". **No norm in HG 1/2016 for Titlul IV, Cap. II contains the word „autoturism" at all** (zero occurrences in the 42,814-character chapter). The norms therefore do **not** say whether the cap attaches to a designated car, to the first car, to the most expensive one, or to a taxpayer's free election. Nothing in the Code or the norms read settles it.

Note the two limits are distinct and cumulative: alin. (5) lit. j) restricts *which one car* may carry costs at all; alin. (7) lit. k) then removes **50 %** of the costs of a motor vehicle not used *exclusively* for the activity (≤ 3.500 kg MMA, ≤ 9 seats, owned or in use), amortisation excluded.

## 6. The protocol cap base — art. 68 alin. (6) — and the order of application

### Answer
**Which categories are excluded from the base: exactly two — private scholarships and protocol.** Nothing else. Both lit. a) (burse private, 5 %) and lit. b) (protocol, 2 %) are computed on the *same* base „determinată conform alin. (6)".

**Order of application: there is no statutory and no normative rule on order.** A full-text scan of the consolidated norms for Titlul IV, Cap. II returns **zero** occurrences of „protocol", of „ordinea", and of „în ordine". Nothing in art. 68 or in pct. 7 of the norms prescribes a sequence among the limited-deductibility items.

**What the text alone permits.** Because alin. (6) removes protocol and burse private *entirely* from the base, and those two are the only capped items whose own amounts are removed, the protocol ceiling and the scholarship ceiling are computed on one and the same base and cannot feed back into each other. **No iteration is required for the protocol/scholarship pair — a single pass suffices.** Whether the protocol base is struck *before* or *after* the other capped items of alin. (5) lit. c)–s) have been cut down is **not settled**: alin. (6) says „venitul brut și **cheltuielile deductibile**", and the Code never says whether „cheltuielile deductibile" here means the amounts as recorded or the amounts *after* the other art. 68 alin. (5) ceilings. The word „deductibile" read together with the heading „Următoarele cheltuieli sunt deductibile limitat" supports the post-cap reading, but that is interpretation, not a rule stated in a primary source.

**Consequence for the app:** the protocol computation is a **single two-pass** (base, then 2 % ceiling) under either reading; it never needs iteration. The only open variable is the composition of the base with respect to the other capped items.

### Verbatim Romanian
> „(5) Următoarele cheltuieli sunt deductibile limitat:
> a) cheltuielile pentru acordarea de burse private, efectuate conform legii, în limita unei cote de 5% din baza de calcul determinată conform alin. (6);
> b) cheltuielile de protocol, în limita unei cote de 2% din baza de calcul determinată conform alin. (6);" — art. 68 alin. (5) lit. a)–b) CF

## 7. FY2026 currency of these caps; OUG 8/2026 and Legea 239/2025

### Answer
**All of the letters are in force for FY2026**: alin. (5) lit. a), b), c), d), e), f), g), **g^1)**, **g^2)**, h), i), j), k), m), n), o), p), q), r), **s)**. Lit. **l) is abrogată** (OUG 18/2018, MO 260/23.03.2018).

**OUG 8/2026 changed the *content* of three letters and added three, but changed no cap amount.** 400 euro stays 400 euro; 100 euro (lit. r) and 4.000 euro (lit. p) were not touched at all. Specifically, art. 6 of OUG 8/2026:
- **pct. 26** — rewrites art. 68 alin. (5) lit. g) (adds „administrate de către entități autorizate … ori de către entități autorizate într-un stat aderent la codurile de liberalizare ale Organizației pentru Cooperare și Dezvoltare Economică");
- **pct. 27** — introduces **lit. g^1)** (pensii ocupaționale, Legea 1/2020) and **lit. g^2)** (PEPP, Regulamentul (UE) 2019/1.238), each at 400 euro anual pentru fiecare persoană;
- **pct. 28** — introduces **lit. s)** (ETF, 400 euro anual pentru fiecare persoană, with a mandatory justificative document).

**The 1 March 2026 claim is confirmed, with a nuance.** OUG 8/2026 art. 10 alin. (1) makes art. 6 effective on publication (25.02.2026) *except* punctele 25–44, which enter into force on **1 martie 2026**. Art. 10 alin. (6) then says pct. 26 and 27 apply „începând cu veniturile aferente anului 2026", while art. 10 alin. (10) says that for **ocupaționale and PEPP** pct. 27 (and 30, 33, 37) apply „**pentru contribuțiile plătite începând cu data de 1 martie 2026**". So for FY2026 the letters g^1)/g^2) operate within the 2026 tax year but only capture **contributions paid from 1 March 2026**. Art. 10 alin. (9) partially compensates for pensii facultative: for 2026, amounts paid **1 ianuarie–28 februarie 2026** are also counted against the 400 euro limit. For ETF (lit. s), art. 10 alin. (7) applies it „începând cu veniturile aferente anului 2026 **pentru sumele plătite și decontate începând cu data de 1 martie 2026**".

**Legea 239/2025 changed nothing in the caps.** Its only touch on art. 68 is art. XII pct. 4, amending **alin. (1)** to add art. 68^3 to the exception list; the Portal Legislativ note records it as dated 01-01-2026 and applicable „începând cu veniturile aferente anului 2026". No note in the 08.08.2026 consolidated form attaches Legea 239/2025 to alin. (5) or alin. (6).

## What the app may compute automatically
1. **The euro→lei conversion for every art. 68 euro cap** (lit. g, g^1, g^2, h, p, r, s): **BNR average rate for the year in which the payment was made** — HG 1/2016 Norme pct. 7 alin. (6). Base and rate are both fully determined, *provided* the app records the payment date/year per expense.
2. **The year against which each euro cap is measured**: the fiscal year = calendar year (art. 65 alin. (1)), with expenses attributed to the year of payment (art. 68 alin. (4) lit. b)) — so a cross-record annual roll-up keyed on payment year is correct.
3. **The per-person flag per letter**: „pentru fiecare persoană" present in g, g^1, g^2, h, r, s; absent in p (4.000 euro anual, single annual amount) and q.
4. **The 5 % contribuții profesionale ceiling** (lit. q): 5 % × venitul brut realizat as defined by art. 68 alin. (2)–(3) of the same article. Single, non-per-person.
5. **The cheltuieli sociale ceiling** (lit. c): 5 % × annual salary expenses of personnel; with zero personnel the ceiling is zero.
6. **The protocol and burse-private ceilings and their base** (alin. (5) lit. a)–b) + alin. (6)): base = venitul brut − cheltuielile deductibile, excluding burse private and protocol; 2 % and 5 % of that same base. **Single two-pass, no iteration** — protocol and burse cannot feed back into the base.
7. **The 50 % vehicle haircut** (alin. (7) lit. k) + pct. 7 alin. (7)): 50 % of the costs of a ≤3.500 kg, ≤9-seat motor vehicle not used exclusively for the activity, amortisation excluded; the listed direct costs (local taxes, RCA, ITP, rovinieta) are inside the limited pool; full deductibility requires justificative documents **plus** a foaie de parcurs with the four mandatory fields.
8. **FY2026 letter inventory and dates**: all letters above in force for FY2026; g^1)/g^2) capture only contributions **paid from 1 March 2026**; lit. s) only amounts **paid and settled from 1 March 2026**; for pensii facultative in 2026 the 400 euro test also counts payments made **1 ianuarie–28 februarie 2026**.

## What must stay unresolved
1. **Which conversion rule governs the euro caps.** HG 1/2016 pct. 7 alin. (6) (BNR **annual average** for the payment year) vs. art. 9 alin. (3) lit. b) + alin. (4) CF (BNR rate **on the payment date**, excepted only by rules „prevăzute expres în prezentul cod"). Missing fact: no primary text declares the norm's average-rate rule to be an express exception to art. 9. **The app must surface the choice, not pick it.**
2. **Whether „pentru fiecare persoană" multiplies by insured/covered persons** (family members under lit. g, h, s). Missing fact: no primary source states whether the cap is per taxpayer or per covered person; art. 68 alin. (7) lit. a) pulls the other way for family use.
3. **Which car carries the „un singur autoturism" cap when the taxpayer has two or more.** Missing fact: the norms for Titlul IV Cap. II never use the word „autoturism"; no designation, ordering or election rule exists. Registrul-inventar identifies vehicles generally (pct. 7 alin. (8)) but does not select one.
4. **Whether „cheltuielile deductibile" in art. 68 alin. (6) is measured before or after the other alin. (5) caps.** Missing fact: no statutory or normative definition; this changes the protocol/burse base but not the number of passes.
5. **Whether art. 68 alin. (4) lit. i) (uncapped) or art. 68 alin. (5) lit. q) (5 % cap) governs contribuții profesionale obligatorii.** Missing fact: no norm resolves the overlap; the norms' illustrative deductible list omits the category entirely.
6. **The entry-into-force dates of Legea 227/2015 itself and of HG 1/2016.** Missing fact: not stated in the consolidated texts read. Only publication dates are verified (MO 688/10.09.2015; MO 22/13.01.2016). Do not hard-code an entry-into-force date for either act.
7. **Whether HG 1/2016 pct. 7 alin. (6) has ever been amended.** The 31.03.2026 consolidated form carries **no amendment note on alin. (6)** (the only note on pct. 7 in that chapter is HG 354/2018, which added alin. (1^1)), so it is treated as original — but this is an inference from the absence of a note, not a positive statement in a primary source.
