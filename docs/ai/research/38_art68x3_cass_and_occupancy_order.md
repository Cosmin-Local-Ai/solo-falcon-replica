## 0. A correction to the citation this pass was asked with

## 1. CASS — the 68^3 net is the base, and the hypothesised omission does not exist

**FACT.** Legea 239/2025 art. XII **pct. 19** amended **art. 170 alin. (1)** to name art. 68^3 expressly, applicable from 2026 incomes:

> „…datorează contribuția de asigurări sociale de sănătate la o bază anuală de calcul egală cu suma rezultată prin cumularea venitului net anual realizat/brut sau normei anuale de venit, respectiv a normei anuale de venit ajustate, după caz, **stabilite potrivit art. 68, 68^1, 68^3 și 69** … care nu poate fi mai mare decât cea corespunzătoare unei baze anuale de calcul egale cu nivelul de **72 de salarii minime brute pe țară**. La determinarea bazei … nu se iau în considerare pierderile fiscale anuale prevăzute la art. 118."

**FACT — the negative finding.** The CAS and CASS amendments were made **in the same law, two puncte apart**: pct. 18 → art. 148 alin. (3) (CAS), pct. 19 → art. 170 alin. (1) (CASS). Both list art. 68^3. A cross-reference scan of the whole consolidation returns **exactly seven** occurrences of „68^3": art. 67 alin. (1^1), art. 68 alin. (1), art. 68^3 itself, art. 83 alin. (4), art. 85 alin. (1), art. 148 alin. (3), art. 170 alin. (1). **There is no omission.**

**FACT — the asymmetry that does exist, and it is structural:**

| | what the 68^3 net does |
|---|---|
| **CAS** (art. 148) | feeds **only the threshold test** in alin. (3). The **base** is „venitul ales de contribuabil" — never below 12 SM, or 24 SM (alin. (2)) |
| **CASS** (art. 170 alin. (1)) | **is the base**, floored at 6 SM (art. 174 alin. (6)), capped at 72 SM, rate 10 % (art. 156) |

**FACT — the 6-SM floor is measured on that base.** Art. 174 alin. (6) tests „baza de calcul prevăzută la art. 170 alin. (1)", which by its own terms includes 68^3. Below 6 SM, the taxpayer owes CASS up to the 6-SM level and files the DU by 25 May.

**FACT — the 6/12/24-SM ladder does not apply to 68^3.** Art. 170 alin. (2)–(4) is expressly limited to art. 155 alin. (1) **lit. c)–h)**. 68^3 income is **lit. b)** (via art. 67 alin. (1^1), inserted by L239/2025 pct. 3). So for 68^3: **base = net, floor = 6 SM, cap = 72 SM, rate = 10 %**.

**FACT — the floor exemption, art. 174 alin. (7).** The top-up is not owed if the person had (a) salary income ≥ 6 SM, (b) lit. c)–h) income bearing CASS ≥ 6 SM, or (c) pension income (lit. c) added by Legea 141/2025 art. II pct. 34, MO 699/25.07.2025). Art. 174 alin. (8) extends alin. (7) to art. 154 alin. (1) lit. a), b), e), f) exempt persons and art. 180 alin. (1) optants.

**FACT — a taxpayer whose only income is 68^3.** (1) base = 70 % × gross; (2) if below 6 SM, art. 174 alin. (6) forces it up; (3) **none of the three alin. (7) exceptions can fire** — no salary, no lit. c)–h) income, no pension — so **the top-up is owed**; (4) DU due 25 May. Art. 154 alin. (1) lit. a) and b) do not help: each closes with „Dacă realizează venituri din cele prevăzute la art. 155 alin. (1) lit. a), b) și f), pentru aceste venituri datorează contribuție".

**FACT — „impozitul fiind final" does not touch CASS.** It sits in art. 68^3 alin. (6), inside Titlul IV, and its subject is „Impozitul anual datorat". Three textual reasons: art. 170 alin. (1) names 68^3 expressly; art. 174 alin. (1) carves out only art. 68^1 from self-assessed CASS; and **comparatively**, art. 68^1 also ends „impozitul fiind final" yet art. 174 alin. (5) makes the payer withhold CASS on it. **Not a live ambiguity. CASS applies.**

**FACT — a stale list to avoid.** ANAF DGRFP Brașov (29.01.2026) gives the art. 154 alin. (1) letters as „a)–c) și e)–g)". The current art. 174 alin. (8) says „a), b), e) și f)", because Legea 141/2025 abrogated several. **Do not encode the ANAF slide's letter list.**

## 2. The occupancy sheet — published, and late

**FACT.** **ORDIN nr. 381 din 24 martie 2026** pentru aprobarea modelului, conținutului și modalității de gestionare a formularului **„Fișa de ocupare a capacității de cazare"**, **MO Partea I nr. 250 din 31 martie 2026** (Președintele ANAF, Adrian Nicușor Nica). Portal Legislativ `DetaliiDocument/308908`.

**FACT — the deadline and the overrun.** Legea 239/2025 art. XIV alin. (2): the order „se emite în termen de **90 de zile** de la data publicării … a prezentei legi", referring to art. XII **pct. 10** (the rewrite of art. 85). Law published 15.12.2025 → due **15 March 2026**. Issued **24 March** (9 days late), published **31 March** (16 days late). **UNKNOWN:** whether the overrun carries any consequence; no sanction provision found.

**FACT — scope and abrogation.** Art. 3: used by contributors with accommodation-services income **or** short-term rental of rooms in personal dwellings, **from 2026**. Art. 6: abrogates **OPANAF 398/2021**.

**FACT — what the model records.** Anul (4 digits); Secțiunea I imobil address; Secțiunea II per-room identifiers and a table with guest name, **guest CNP** (or ID series/number where there is no CNP), first day, last day. **One form per room / per amenaged space.** Short-term is defined as uninterrupted rental to the same person for **at most 30 days** in a calendar year, and **a fraction of a day counts as a day**. Beneficiaries: 1–7 rooms in personal dwellings (regardless of how many dwellings), plus independent-activity accommodation and >7 rooms.

**FACT — it is a retention obligation, not a filing.** „să completeze și să păstreze"; one copy, kept by the taxpayer. Neither the order nor art. 68^3 alin. (8) requires submission.

**FACT — a live inconsistency.** The **D212 instructions (OPANAF 2736/2025, MO 1207/29.12.2025)** still instruct the tourist-rental subsection to use „Fișa capacității de cazare", **aprobat prin OPANAF nr. 398/2021** — an order **abrogated** by OPANAF 381/2026. The instructions predate it.

## 3. art. 68^3 alin. (1)–(12), read in full

Source: consolidation 08.08.2026. Only amendment note: added by L239/2025 art. XII pct. 5, from 2026 incomes. **No later amendment.**

- **alin. (1)** — scope: „operatorii economici definiți potrivit legislației specifice domeniului turismului – contribuabili potrivit prezentului titlu", by making available a space fitted for overnight stays for a period measured in days. **AMBIGUOUS / INTERPRETATION.** The gate is a legal classification outside the fiscal code (Legea 97/2020 / OG 21/2015, ONRC authorisation). Reading A: only entities qualifying as „operator economic" under tourism law. Reading B: any Titlul IV payer providing such accommodation. ANAF's Brașov material leans to A. **Not determinable from the fiscal code — the app can ask, not decide.**
- **alin. (2)** — net = gross − 30 % × gross = **70 % × gross**. Gross is **cash basis** („încasate în cursul anului fiscal"). Gross-inclusion rules borrowed from art. 68 alin. (2) lit. a), b), d), e) and alin. (3) — **lit. c) is deliberately omitted.**
- **alin. (3)** — „**Nu se cuprinde în venitul brut comisionul reținut de către entitățile care facilitează închirierea pe termen scurt a camerelor situate în locuințe proprietate personală**…". **AMBIGUOUS — the highest-risk figure in the module.** The words are limited to rooms in personal dwellings, yet the alineat sits in an article whose alin. (1) subject is a tourism operator's space. **Reading A (narrow):** the exclusion bites only via alin. (12) / art. 83 alin. (4); a hotel's gross is the full amount received. **Reading B (broad):** a general gross rule for all 68^3 income. ANAF Brașov leans B; the statutory words are A. **It changes gross, and therefore tax, CASS base and the CAS threshold. Must not be silently resolved.**
- **alin. (4)** — foreign currency converted at the **BNR rate of the last banking day before each collection**. Not an annual average, not year-end.
- **alin. (5)** — part-year and multiple part-year sums **se consideră venit anual**. **No proration** — contrast art. 69, where the normă is adjusted proportionally.
- **alin. (6)** — 10 % on the net, „impozitul fiind final" → **effective 7 % of gross**.
- **alin. (7)** — only the **income part** of the fiscal evidence register; **no bookkeeping duties**. Register for independent activities governed by OMFP 3254/2017 (MO 10/05.01.2018); OMF 808/2026 (MO 556/07.07.2026) replaced OMFP 870/2005 and covers only the profit-tax register. **UNKNOWN:** OMFP 3254/2017 not verified against a primary text in this pass, and no 68^3-specific register model was found.
- **alin. (8)** — the occupancy sheet, now OPANAF 381/2026.
- **alin. (9)–(10)** — self-assessed and **paid** via the DU, both by the art. 122 alin. (3) term, **25 May**. Cite art. 68^3 alin. (10) + art. 122 alin. (3), **not art. 121**.
- **alin. (11)** — if the same year mixes accommodation with normă or real-system activity, **art. 68 and 69 still apply to those**. **The ring-fence is an income-tax rule only**: for CASS, art. 170 alin. (1) **cumulates** across all lit. b) sources, and art. 148 alin. (3) does the same for CAS. **Separate for tax, combined for contributions.**
- **alin. (12)** — applies also to art. 83 alin. (4): from the fiscal year in which short-term rooms in a personal dwelling exceed **7**, the income is an independent activity under 68^3.

## 4. What the app can and cannot build on this

**Buildable, deterministic, primary-sourced:**
1. Income tax: gross (cash receipts, FX per alin. (4)) → net = 70 % × gross → 10 % tax, final.
2. CASS: the 68^3 net, cumulated with other lit. b) income, floor 6 SM, cap 72 SM, rate 10 %.
3. CAS: the net feeds the 12/24-SM threshold test; base 12 or 24 SM; rate 25 %.
4. **FY2026 constants:** SM **4.050 lei** — the 1 January value, **not** the 4.325 lei from 1 July (HG 146/2026), because **art. 135^1 alin. (3)** fixes the 1-January value for art. 148, 151, 170, 174 and 174^1 „indiferent dacă în cursul aceluiași an se utilizează mai multe valori". 6 SM = 24.300; 12 SM = 48.600; 24 SM = 97.200; 72 SM = 291.600; min CASS 2.430; max CASS 29.160. **Already correct in the repo:** `package2026.ts` pins 4.050 and cites art. 135^1 alin. (3).
5. The sole-68^3-income case: the 6-SM minimum **is** owed.
6. Deadlines: filing and payment both 25 May.

**Must stay `review_required`:**
- **alin. (3) commission scope** — two live readings, no primary resolution. Surface it as an explicit either/or, never a default.
- **alin. (1) eligibility gate** — a tourism-law classification. Ask the user; do not decide.
- **art. 174 alin. (7) floor exemptions** — need salary, pension and lit. c)–h) data the module does not hold. The app may say "the floor applies unless other income exists", never "no floor".
- **D212 cell mapping** — OPANAF 2736/2025 has **no 68^3 subsection** and cites the abrogated 398/2021. Do not derive a form map from it.
- **Multi-year** — FY2027 SM unknown; every 24.300 / 291.600 must be year-scoped.

## 6. One product question this raises

The fișă requires the **CNP of every guest** plus stay dates, per room, retained by the taxpayer. If the app reproduces the form it becomes a holder of guest personal data with **no ANAF filing counterpart** — retention only. Recommend the app record **occupancy counts and nights** for its own purposes and leave guest CNP on the taxpayer's own sheet. **This is a decision for the user, not an implementation detail.**
