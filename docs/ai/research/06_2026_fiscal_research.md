# 2026 Fiscal Research — PFA Dashboard

Research document for a 2026 tax dashboard covering Romanian PFA (persoană fiză autorizată) taxation.

**Scope:** Rules applicable to tax year 2026 for a PFA operating under the actual system (sistem real), including income tax, social contributions (CAS/CASS), D212 filing/payment deadlines, the 300,000 lei small-entrepreneur VAT exemption, and 2026-specific changes (minimum wage, thresholds).

**Method:** Internet research only, using authoritative Romanian sources as final authority (ANAF, legislatie.just.ro, Monitorul Oficial, official Government/Ministry publications). Generic tax websites were used only to locate official sources.

**Field legend (used for every rule):** Rule / Legal source / Article-section / URL / Publication date / Effective date / Tax-year applicability / Confidence / Ambiguity.

## 1. PFA Sistem Real, Impozit pe Venit, Cheltuieli Deductibile, Baze de Calcul

### 1.1 PFA income — definition and classification

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Rule** | Income from a PFA (persoană fiză autorizată) is classified as "venituri din activități independente" (income from independent activities), which includes income from business activities (activități de întreprindere) carried out by natural persons, including PFA. This is the income category taxed in the real system (sistem real) by default. |
| **Legal source** | Codul fiscal (Law 227/2015), as amended |
| **Article/section** | Art. 67 (definition of independent-activity income); Art. 68 (sistem real) |
| **URL** | https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/3671 |

| **Effective date** | 1 Jan 2016 |
| **Tax-year applicability** | General (including 2026) |
| **Confidence** | High |
| **Ambiguity** | None — PFA business income is unambiguously in the "activități independente" category, taxed in sistem real unless the forfetary option (Art. 69¹) is exercised. |

### 1.2 Sistem real — how PFA income is calculated

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Rule** | Under the real system (sistem real), the net taxable income of a PFA is calculated as **total revenue (venituri brute) minus deductible expenses (cheltuieli deductibile)**. The PFA is the default regime; the forfetary system (sistem forfetar, Art. 69¹) is an optional alternative. |
| **Legal source** | Codul fiscal (Law 227/2015), as amended |
| **Article/section** | Art. 68(1)–(2) |
| **URL** | https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/3671 |

| **Effective date** | 1 Jan 2016 |
| **Tax-year applicability** | General (including 2026) |
| **Confidence** | High |
| **Ambiguity** | None on the core formula. The exact forfetary-option mechanics (Art. 69¹) are out of scope for this batch. |

### 1.3 Deductible expenses — what qualifies, documentation, limits

| Field | Value |

**Rule B — 16% progressive rate on high income (applies to 2026):**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

### 1.5 Advance payments and annual settlement (relevant to calculation bases)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Rule** | PFA earning independent-activity income must make **advance payments (plăți anticipate)** during the year. They are set by the tax authority (ANAF) based on the estimated annual income or the net income from the previous year, and are paid in **4 equal installments by the 25th of the last month of each quarter** (25 March, 25 June, 25 September, 25 December). New taxpayers must file a declaration of estimated income within **15 days** of starting the activity. The **annual tax** is settled via the tax assessment decision (decizia de impunere) on the basis of the actual net income declared in the **Declarația unică**, filed by **25 May** of the following year; advance payments already paid are credited against the annual tax. |
| **Legal source** | Codul fiscal (Law 227/2015), as amended |
| **Article/section** | Art. 81 (advance payments); Art. 83(1) (annual declaration, 25 May deadline) |
| **URL** | https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/3671 |
| **Publication date** | 22 Dec 2015 |
| **Effective date** | 1 Jan 2016 |
| **Tax-year applicability** | General (including 2026) |
| **Confidence** | High on the 4-installment/25th-of-quarter rule and the 25 May annual deadline; medium on the exact paragraph numbers within Art. 81/83 |
| **Ambiguity** | Note: in the older 2003 code (Law 571/2003) these rules sat in Art. 82; in the current Law 227/2015 numbering they are in Art. 81 (advance payments) and Art. 83 (annual declaration). The exact paragraph numbers should be confirmed in a dedicated batch. |

### 1.6 Calculation base — net taxable income and loss carry-forward

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Rule** | The **net taxable income (venitul net anual impozabil)** is computed as total income from each category minus the deductible expenses, aggregated across sources. **Fiscal losses** (negative net income) can be **carried forward for up to 5 consecutive years**, limited to **50% of the net income** of each subsequent year in which the loss is deducted. |
| **Legal source** | Codul fiscal (Law 227/2015), as amended |
| **Article/section** | Art. 118(1) (net taxable income); Art. 118(4)–(5) (loss carry-forward, 5 years, 50% cap) |

### Minimum-wage distinction (general vs special statutory references)

- **GENERAL minimum wage (salariul minim brut pe economie):** 4,050 lei/month from 1 Jan 2026 (HG 1506/2024, art. 164 Codul muncii); raised to 4,325 lei/month from 1 July 2026 by HG 146/2026. This is the labor-law floor for wages — it is NOT directly a tax threshold.
- **SPECIAL statutory references used by tax rules:** CASS/CAS minimum base (6 SM), CASS maximum base (72 SM from 2026 per Legea 239/2025), CAS maximum base (24 SM) — all are multiples of the minimum wage **in force on 1 January of the income year (4,050 lei for 2026)**. The mid-year (July 2026) wage increase does NOT change 2026 tax thresholds.
- Do NOT assume every threshold derives from the currently active general minimum wage: e.g., the 16% income-tax threshold (6 × national average gross salary, Batch 1 §1.4) is derived from the **average salary**, not the minimum wage.

# IMPLEMENTATION NOTES FOR WORKERS

- **This document is the fiscal starting specification.** It defines the verified 2026 baseline and the modeling constraints for the dashboard's fiscal logic.
- **Workers must not invent unsupported tax rules.** Any rule not covered by the verified sources in this document is out of scope — no speculative formulas, rates, thresholds, or deadlines.
- **Unresolved cases must return `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.** Any case not covered by a verified rule (including the "Unresolved / requires review" items above) must surface `REVIEW_REQUIRED` rather than a guessed result.
- **Tax logic belongs in a deterministic domain layer.** Fiscal rules are data-driven domain rules; calculations are pure and deterministic, decoupled from the UI.
- **Dashboard components consume calculation results rather than calculating taxes.** Components render outputs produced by the domain layer; they never compute taxes, contributions, or deadlines inline.
