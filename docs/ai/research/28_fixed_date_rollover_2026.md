# R-A — Does a fixed statutory fiscal date roll when it falls on a non-working day? (2026-10-10)

**Researcher:** `scout-R-a-rollover`. Transcribed by the Orchestrator (**Q-RESEARCHER-WRITE**). Report arrived **complete**, not truncated.
**Question:** CF art. 122 alin. (3) fixes the Declarația Unică deadline as „până la data de 25 mai inclusiv". When such a **fixed calendar date** falls on a Saturday, Sunday or holiday, does it move — and by what authority?
**Why it was asked:** `Q-ROLLOVER-FIXED-DATE` had evidence on both sides and blocks any working-day primitive in Phases 18B, 24, 32. A wrong answer here costs a real user a real penalty.

**Headline: settled as practice, unsettled as authority.** ANAF itself has repeatedly published the rolled date — centrally in 2025, by county in 2024 and 2026 — and once stated the rollover rule in general terms. **No normative instrument, no MF order and no ÎCCJ decision has ever stated the authority for it.** The available hook (CPF art. 75 → CPC art. 180 alin. (2) / art. 181 alin. (2)) is doctrinally plausible and untested for fixed dates; CPF art. 76 alin. (2) and OUG 15/2019's preamble point the other way.

---

## 1. ANAF's own record — the pattern, deadline by deadline

**2025 — 25 mai was a Sunday. The central ANAF press office published the rolled date, and ANAF's brochure stated the rule.** FACT.
- **ANAF central communiqué nr. 521/19.05.2025** (Serviciul comunicare, relații publice și mass media): „Declarația unică se depune până la data de 26 mai, inclusiv. Termenul limită pentru depunerea Declarației unice (Formular 212) este 26 mai 2025 (inclusiv), pentru veniturile obținute în anul precedent." [PDF](https://static.anaf.ro/static/3/Anaf/20250519123202_com_du2025.pdf) — a „moves to a named date" statement, **no legal basis cited**.
- **ANAF's DU 2025 informational material goes further and states the rule:** „Întrucât data de 25.05.2025 este zi nelucrătoare (duminica), **termenul pentru îndeplinirea obligației de depunere se prelungește până în prima zi lucrătoare următoare - 26.05.2025**" — [web_DU.pdf](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Brasov/Brasov/web_DU.pdf), [DU_inv.pdf](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Brasov/Brasov/DU_inv.pdf), [DU_Activ_Indep.pdf](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Brasov/Brasov/DU_Activ_Indep.pdf). The closest thing to ANAF stating the term **is** extended, with no instrument invoked.
- DGRFP Ploiești: „26 MAI 2025, TERMEN PENTRU DEPUNEREA DECLARAȚIEI UNICE". DGRFP Brașov, 09.05.2025: „termenul limită până la care pot depune Declaraţia unică (formular 212) este 26 mai 2025".

**2024 — 25 mai was a Saturday. Two county units used the extension formula verbatim; no instrument existed.** FACT for the statements.
- DGRFP Brașov (republished 22.05.2024): „Termenul legal de depunere a declaraţiei unice este 25 mai 2024 care, **împlinindu-se într-o zi nelucrătoare, se prelungeşte până la data de 27 mai 2024**." AJFP Teleorman: identical sentence. Note the grammar — „se prelungește" (automatic), not „a fost prelungit prin…". No OUG, no MF order, no OPANAF found moving the 2024 term.

**2026 — 28 februarie was a Saturday: a *different* fixed statutory date rolled by an ANAF notice. The cleanest recent precedent.** FACT.
- **DGRFP Cluj-Napoca, comunicat 20.02.2026:** „termenul limită de depunere a formularului 205 … pentru anul 2025, **este 2 martie 2026, având în vedere că zilele de 28 februarie și 1 martie sunt zile nelucrătoare**." [PDF](https://static.anaf.ro/static/3/Cluj/20260220113603_cj_d205_20feb2026.pdf). The statutory date is „până în ultima zi a lunii februarie inclusiv a anului curent pentru anul expirat" (CF art. 132 alin. (2)) — a pure fixed date, rolled to Monday by a county notice, no instrument.

**2019 is the counter-case, and it is distinguishable.** FACT. OUG 15/2019 (MO 204/14.03.2019, PL **211826**), art. unic alin. (1) prorogated the DU term from 15 martie to 31 iulie 2019; the preamble says „prorogarea acestui termen nu se poate realiza decât printr-un **act normativ de același nivel**". **But 15 martie 2019 was a Tuesday** — the OUG was about SPV overload and instrument hierarchy, not weekends. It is evidence about *how the legislator* moves a fixed date, not evidence that rollover does not apply. ANAF's companion note titled „**EXTINDEREA** TERMENULUI … până la data de 31 iulie 2019" (BAZA LEGALĂ: OUG 15/2019) shows ANAF itself distinguishing an instrument-based extension from an automatic one.

**Silence where it matters most for the app: the 25th-of-month obligations.** FACT (absence found). For **25 iulie 2026 (Saturday)** and **25 octombrie 2026 (Sunday)** — D100/D112/D300/D390 and the CF „până la data de 25 inclusiv a lunii următoare" family — **no ANAF/DGRFP statement at all**. The rollover assertion for those dates appears only in private fiscal calendars (REALCONT, Eurocont, Contera, Regnet, LawPedia, Omega Fiscal), citing CPF art. 75 or „dreptul comun". Silence is not permission, and not refusal either.

## 2. Authority — nothing normative, and the nearest court decision cuts the other way

**No RIL and no hotărâre prealabilă on this point was found.** NONE FOUND — not proven absence. Searches covered „Decizia RIL" + termen + zi nelucrătoare + termen legal fix, „hotărâre prealabilă" + CF/CPF + termen, and ÎCCJ decisions on fixed fiscal filing dates.

**The nearest ÎCCJ authority cuts *against* automatic application of CPC art. 181 alin. (2) where a special regime governs the term.** FACT. **Decizia nr. 8/2024** (RIL, Avocatul Poporului), **MO 573 din 19.06.2024**: in interpreting CM art. 75 alin. (1) and art. 278 alin. (1), the pre-notice term runs from the day after notification and is fulfilled on the last day, „**dispoziţiile art. 181 alin. (1) pct. 2 şi alin. (2) din Codul de procedură civilă, precum şi cele ale art. 2.553 alin. (1) din Codul civil nefiind aplicabile**". Reasoning at paras. 77–78: a term composed only of working days „nu poate începe, nu poate include şi nu se poate împlini într-o zi nelucrătoare". This is the closest analogue to the CPF art. 75 gate („dacă legislația fiscală nu dispune altfel") being used to **switch off** art. 181 alin. (2). It is not about fixed calendar dates, so it does not decide our question — but it shows the gate is real and can close.

**The CPC hook exists and is used in MF-adjacent writing, unresolved.** INTERPRETATION. CPC (Legea 134/2010, republicat) art. 180 alin. (2): „În cazurile prevăzute de lege, termenul este reprezentat de data la care se îndeplinește un anumit act de procedură"; art. 181 alin. (2): „**Când ultima zi a unui termen cade într-o zi nelucrătoare, termenul se prelungește până în prima zi lucrătoare care urmează.**" The **Revista Ministerului Finanțelor Publice nr. 3/2023, „Termenele în procedura fiscală"** ([PDF](https://mfinante.gov.ro/static/10/Mfp/revista/articolnr3.pdf)) reproduces exactly this chain (CPC art. 180–186 via CPF art. 75) as the regime for fiscal terms — but does not squarely decide whether a fixed statutory date is a „termen" for art. 181 alin. (2). A journal article, not a normative act.

**The CPF itself supplies an instrument route for moving a fiscal deadline — and it is not a press release.** FACT. CPF (Legea 207/2015), Titlul III, Cap. VI „Termene", art. 75–78:
- **art. 75** — „În ceea ce privește calculul termenelor se aplică dispozițiile Codului de procedură civilă, republicat, în materie, **dacă legislația fiscală nu dispune altfel**."
- **art. 76 alin. (1)** — terms set by law and administered by a fiscal organ may be extended by that organ, „în situații temeinic justificate, la cerere sau din oficiu".
- **art. 76 alin. (2)** — „Ori de câte ori legislația fiscală prevede un termen … acesta poate fi prelungit/modificat, pentru motive justificate ce țin de activitatea de administrare a creanțelor fiscale, **prin ordin al ministrului finanțelor publice**."
Plus CF art. 3 lit. b) — „certitudinea impunerii … **termenele**, modalitatea și sumele de plată să fie **precis stabilite**".

**INTERPRETATION:** read together, CPF art. 76 alin. (2) and OUG 15/2019's preamble suggest that where the *date itself* must change, an instrument is required — while art. 181 alin. (2) via art. 75 is a *calculation* rule, which is precisely how ANAF's 2025 brochure treats it. That is the seam the whole question sits in, and no authority has closed it.

## 3. The 25 mai 2025 case, resolved

FACT. **No instrument moved the DU term in 2025 and the CF date was never touched.** CF art. 122 alin. (3) still reads „până la data de 25 mai inclusiv a anului următor…", confirmed by ANAF's own 2026 material („Declaraţia se depune până la data de 25 mai 2026 inclusiv…", [București](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Bucuresti/Noutati_privind_DU_2026_exemple.pdf)) and by **OPANAF nr. 2.719/2025** (Procedura de precompletare a declarației unice), **MO 1 din 05.01.2026**, PL **306252**, art. 13 alin. (2): „Declarația unică … se depune la organul fiscal competent, **până la data de 25 mai inclusiv a anului următor celui de raportare**." An ANAF order issued in December 2025 restates 25 mai as the operative date; it prorogates nothing. The 2025 DU instruments (OPANAF 1929/30.07.2025, OPANAF 2736/2025) approve form models only.

**So the 2025 shift to 26 mai was effected entirely by communication** — and it was not merely a county press release, as previously recorded here. **The central ANAF press office and the central brochure carried it.** That upgrades the earlier framing in `research/18` §Q2.

## 4. Prospective exposure — the DU is clean, the monthly dates are not

FACT (calendar). **25 mai 2027 is a Tuesday.** 25 mai 2025 = Sunday, 25 mai 2026 = Monday, 25 mai 2027 = Tuesday. The DU needs no rollover handling for either year in the app's window.

**But the app's other fixed dates are live test cases now, in 2026:**

| Date | Day | Obligation family |
|---|---|---|
| 25 iulie 2026 | **Saturday** | D100 / D112 / D300 / D390 — „până la data de 25 inclusiv a lunii următoare" |
| **25 octombrie 2026** | **Sunday** | same — **imminent, and ANAF has published nothing about it** |
| 25 decembrie 2026 | Friday but a **legal holiday** (Crăciun); 26 Dec = Saturday | next working day **28 decembrie** |
| 31 octombrie 2026 | **Saturday** | D406 |
| 30 noiembrie + 1 decembrie 2026 | both legal holidays | D394 / D406 → **2 decembrie** |

**This is the finding that changes the schedule:** the app cannot carry this quietly. It needs a policy for the 25th-of-month dates within weeks, and 25 octombrie 2026 is the first hard case.

## 5. Q-ANAF-SAMBITA — the authority gap is confirmed, not closed

FACT + AUTHORITY GAP. The rule text is now explicit that the Regulation governs the calculation — OUG 120/2021 art. 10 alin. (7) as amended from 01.01.2026, verbatim per ANAF DGRFP Brașov's own comparative table of OUG 89/2025 ([PDF](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Brasov/Brasov/facturare_electronica_89.pdf)): „… **5 zile lucrătoare** de la data emiterii facturii, dar nu mai târziu de 5 zile lucrătoare de la data-limită pentru emiterea facturii prevăzută la art. 319 alin. (16) … **Calculul termenului-limită se efectuează conform Regulamentului (CEE, Euratom) nr. 1182/71**…" (same for art. 10¹ alin. (2¹), B2C).

- **AJFP Argeș, 15.01.2026:** quotes only the Reg. 1182/71 art. 3 alin. (1) start/end rule and is **silent on sâmbăta** — consistent with the known Argeș and Galați answers.
- **MF comunicat 27.08.2024** remains the only explicit MF counting instruction ever issued, and it is anti-rollover — but it addressed the **superseded calendar-day rule**: „expira în cea de-a 5-a zi, **indiferent ca aceasta este o zi lucratoare sau nelucratoare**".
- ANAF's central e-Factura FAQ and the MF Ghid RO e-Factura contain **no working-day counting rule**.

**Finding:** nothing newer or more authoritative than the two AJFP answers exists. The only textual anchor remains **Reg. 1182/71 art. 2 alin. (2)** (Saturday is not a working day), **which no Romanian fiscal authority has applied in print.**

## 7. What the app may and must not say

**P1 — may state, with citation:** that ANAF publicly communicated that a fixed fiscal term falling on a non-working day moves to the next working day — DU 2024 → 27 mai (DGRFP Brașov, AJFP Teleorman), DU 2025 → 26 mai (ANAF central comunicat 521/19.05.2025 + ANAF material informativ), F205 for 2025 → 2 martie 2026 (DGRFP Cluj-Napoca, 20.02.2026). **Must not state** that ANAF has extended a deadline for a date it has not addressed (25 octombrie 2026, 25 decembrie 2026 — silence). The inference from DU/F205 to D112/D300/D390/D406 is **REVIEW_REQUIRED**.

**P2 — must not state** any legal basis for rollover as settled, and **must not state** that rollover is prohibited either. It may state the provision chain (CPF art. 75 → CPC art. 180–181) as text, labelled as an interpretation no court has confirmed for fixed dates. **The rule itself stays REVIEW_REQUIRED.**

**P3 — settled and citable:** no instrument moved the DU term in 2025; CF art. 122 alin. (3) still says 25 mai; the rolled date came from communications only.

**P4:** 25 mai 2027 may be shown as a plain statutory date with no warning. The 2026 25th-of-month dates must be **flagged, not silently rolled**.

**P5:** state the statutory text (5 working days, calculation per Reg. 1182/71) and state that **no ANAF/MF instrument defines whether Saturday counts**. Any Saturday treatment is REVIEW_REQUIRED; the conservative display is the **earlier** of the two candidate deadlines.

**P6:** remove any logic keyed to „art. 174 + OUG 156/2024"; it does not exist.

## 8. The adopted position (researcher's recommendation, accepted by the Orchestrator)

Because a wrong answer produces a real penalty and the authority is genuinely unsettled, the defensible behaviour is **neither automatic rollover nor a bare refusal to roll**:

> **Keep the statutory date as the operative deadline** for every internal calculation, reminder and „you are late" state — because filing on or before the statutory date is never wrong, while relying on an uninstrumented rollover can be — and **surface the rolled date as clearly labelled information, never as the app's own legal conclusion.**

Candidate RO wording for 25 octombrie 2026:

> „Termenul legal este 25 octombrie 2026 (duminică). ANAF a comunicat în cazuri asemănătoare (Declarația Unică 2024 și 2025, Formular 205 pentru 2026) că un termen care cade în zi nelucrătoare se prelungește până în prima zi lucrătoare — aici 26 octombrie 2026 — dar această prelungire nu rezultă dintr-un act normativ și nu a fost confirmată de ANAF pentru această dată. Pentru siguranță, depuneți până vineri, 23 octombrie 2026."

It reports what ANAF actually did, names the units and dates, states the limit of the evidence, gives the user the earlier safe action, and stops short of advice. **This is the same shape as the app's existing conflict disclosure: state the rule used, cite both sides, name the gap, give the safe action.**
