## 5. REVIEW_REQUIRED surfaces a real user could trip today

Each of these is a place where the app knows it is unsure. None may be presented as a settled figure. This list is the honest inventory of the app's uncertainty, and PG-13's job is to confirm each one is *visible* rather than hidden.

- FY2027 selected — no verified 2027 package; 2027 minimum wage unpublished (**D-YEAR-2**: carried forward, stamped as not yet updated). The engine refuses rather than computing on 2026 rules (`tax.ts` `review_required`), and the threshold rows disappear rather than go stale.
- Any euro figure for FY2026 — BNR publishes no FY2026 annual average yet, so every ceiling is **provisional** (B-33 closed by surfacing this, not by resolving it).
- Mid-year starter near the 12-SM CAS plafon — the only band where the code/norm conflict changes the answer (`research/27` §10).
- Expense categories whose art. 68 cap cannot be struck — `cheltuieli sociale` has no euro equivalent and no rate fixes it. These arrive as `cap_unresolved` coverage gaps, and the estimate says *incomplete*, not *Calculat*.
- VAT periodicity — depends on prior-year taxable supplies and the registration date, facts the app does not hold (**D-VAT-1**, Q-VAT-PERIOD).
- **Replaced (was „the depreciation model does not exist (B-31)").** B-31 closed 2026-10-12: the asset register and the computed schedule exist. What is genuinely unsure now is the **collision** — a hand-recorded depreciation expense beside a computed schedule is refused out loud (`depreciation_conflict`, B-93), an asset with a dangling link is named (B-108), and a disposal stops the schedule on a reading the app labels as a reading (B-153, D-DEPR-1).
- **New (B-159).** A receipt marked „Banii nu au intrat încă" or a cost marked „Nu a fost plătită încă" is **out of the base by law**, not by omission — the row says which article keeps it out. A blank money date is a different state: the app reads the document date as the money date and says so out loud (B-67's `assumedFromDocument`, B-38's `fallbackDefault`). Conflating the two is what B-159 was.
- The 25.000-euro sistem-real threshold — anchored to the year of income realisation, a different conversion basis, not implemented.

## 6. Date-boundary readiness

- **Trigger, not a phase** (§12.4 of the plan): stage the 2027 rule package when the 2027 wage and budget acts publish. First hard boundary ≈ twelve weeks out.
- **Fixed clock exists** — PG-0 shipped it: one pinned instant (`src/test/fixedInstant.ts`, 2026-10-10T12:00 local) and one clock seam (`domain/date.ts`), so every date-dependent acceptance criterion is testable. The old line „until then, every 'identical estimate at three dates' criterion is untestable" is spent.
- **UTC date defaults (B-46) are fixed** — closed 2026-10-10, and `wallClockGuard.test.ts` now forbids no-arg `Date` construction in `src/pages/` and `src/components/`, so the class cannot silently return. What remains of B-46 is the *injection* half (phase 32), not the defaults.
- **New, and it belongs here:** B-160's clock half is the mirror image of this section. The app's *tests* now read a pinned clock; the app's *own* `asOfDate` is captured once per session and never re-read, so a dashboard left open across midnight is measuring yesterday.
